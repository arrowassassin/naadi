# 06. Choosing specialties

*Stage key `specialty_choice` · last verified 2026-11-15 · re-verify quarterly and immediately on policy news.*

In 2026, non-US IMGs needing visa sponsorship matched at 54.4%, a five-year low [NRMP](https://www.nrmp.org/about/news/2026/03/nrmp-releases-results-of-the-2026-main-residency-match-for-more-than-38000-future-residents/). Categorical Internal Medicine is by far the largest destination (3,448 non-US IMGs, 30.8% of positions) and Family Medicine is the least score-driven safety [NRMP 2026](https://www.nrmp.org/wp-content/uploads/2026/05/Main_Match_Results_and_Data-2026.pdf). Matched and unmatched IMGs differ by only 5–10 Step 2 CK points but matched IMGs ranked about 2.5× as many programs [NRMP 2024](https://www.nrmp.org/wp-content/uploads/2024/09/Charting_Outcomes_IMG2024.pdf), so interviews are the bottleneck.

## Task details

**Know your baseline odds** (`spec-01`) — In 2026, 11,944 non-US IMGs applied and 56.4% matched to PGY-1 [NRMP 2026](https://www.nrmp.org/wp-content/uploads/2026/05/Main_Match_Results_and_Data-2026.pdf); those needing visa sponsorship matched at 54.4%, a five-year low [NRMP](https://www.nrmp.org/about/news/2026/03/nrmp-releases-results-of-the-2026-main-residency-match-for-more-than-38000-future-residents/). Non-US IMG applicants rose from 7,864 in 2022 [NRMP 2026](https://www.nrmp.org/wp-content/uploads/2026/05/Main_Match_Results_and_Data-2026.pdf). US programs do not use your MBBS percentage or NEET PG rank.

**Make categorical Internal Medicine your primary specialty** (`spec-02`) — Non-US IMGs took 3,448 categorical IM PGY-1 positions in 2026, 30.8% of the total [NRMP 2026](https://www.nrmp.org/wp-content/uploads/2026/05/Main_Match_Results_and_Data-2026.pdf). Matched non-US IMGs in IM averaged Step 2 CK 248 vs 242 for unmatched and ranked 6.1 vs 2.5 contiguous programs (2024) [NRMP 2024](https://www.nrmp.org/wp-content/uploads/2024/09/Charting_Outcomes_IMG2024.pdf).

**Add Family Medicine as a parallel safety** (`spec-03`) — FM gave non-US IMGs 962 positions (17.5%) in 2026 [NRMP 2026](https://www.nrmp.org/wp-content/uploads/2026/05/Main_Match_Results_and_Data-2026.pdf); matched vs unmatched Step 2 CK was 231 vs 227 in 2024 [NRMP 2024](https://www.nrmp.org/wp-content/uploads/2024/09/Charting_Outcomes_IMG2024.pdf). Many community FM programs list no visa sponsorship, so check each one [ResidencyMatch.ai](https://www.residencymatch.ai/img-friendly/by-state/ca).

**Add Peds, Psychiatry, Neurology or Pathology only with real interest** (`spec-04`) — 2026 non-US IMG shares: Pediatrics 21.9%, Psychiatry 8.8%, Neurology 17.4%, Pathology 26.7% [NRMP 2026](https://www.nrmp.org/wp-content/uploads/2026/05/Main_Match_Results_and_Data-2026.pdf). Pathology programs most often report “often” interviewing non-US IMGs (at least 71%) [NRMP 2026 PD Survey](https://www.nrmp.org/wp-content/uploads/2026/08/2026-PD-Survey-Report-Narrative-FINAL.pdf). Each needs specialty-specific letters, so add one only if an observership shows genuine interest.

**Avoid low-IMG fields this cycle** (`spec-05`) — Non-US IMGs filled only 4.4% of Anesthesiology, 7.1% of categorical Surgery, 2.7% of OB-GYN, 2.9% of EM and 2.0% of PM&R PGY-1 positions in 2026 [NRMP 2026](https://www.nrmp.org/wp-content/uploads/2026/05/Main_Match_Results_and_Data-2026.pdf). Only 26.6% of non-US IMGs preferring General Surgery matched to it in 2024 [NRMP 2024](https://www.nrmp.org/wp-content/uploads/2024/09/Charting_Outcomes_IMG2024.pdf).

**Check year-of-graduation filters** (`spec-06`) — “Graduated within 5 years” is a common filter, for example at Highland IM [FREIDA](https://freida.ama-assn.org/program/1400531051) and UCSF-Fresno IM [UCSF Fresno](https://fresno.ucsf.edu/internal-medicine/applicants). If you graduated 4–5 years before the Match, slipping a cycle may put you outside these programs.

**Set your specialty mix by Step 2 CK score band** (`spec-07`) — Naadi estimates (judgment built from NRMP data [NRMP 2024](https://www.nrmp.org/wp-content/uploads/2024/09/Charting_Outcomes_IMG2024.pdf), not NRMP figures): about 250 gives roughly 65–80% IM match chance; about 240 gives 45–60%, or 60–70% with FM in parallel; below 235, apply FM-heavy. Observership-only exposure or a graduation year 4–5 years back lowers each band by about 10–15 points.

**Use NRMP's interactive Step 2 CK report** (`spec-08`) — NRMP's Step 2 CK baseline report lets you filter by non-US IMG and specialty to see matched and unmatched counts by score [NRMP](https://www.nrmp.org/match-data/2026/07/charting-outcomes-usmle-step-2-ck-exam-baseline-2/). The 2026 non-US IMG Charting Outcomes report (Jul 2026) is now annual [NRMP](https://www.nrmp.org/match-data/2026/07/charting-outcomes-characteristics-of-non-u-s-citizen-international-medical-students-graduates-who-matched-to-their-preferred-specialty-2026-main-residency-match/).

**Decide your fallback trigger for a later Match** (`spec-09`) — Switch to the next cycle if Step 1 is not safely passed by about end of March of the application year, Step 2 CK cannot be sat by about July in your target range, or no B-1 or observership is possible before August. The cost is one more year since graduation against “within 5 years” filters [UCSF Fresno](https://fresno.ucsf.edu/internal-medicine/applicants). Do documented clinical work in India to avoid an unexplained gap.

## Naadi match-chance estimates (judgment, not NRMP figures)

Assumes first-attempt passes, 4–8 weeks of observership with US letters, recent graduation and broad applications.

| Step 2 CK | IM match chance | Strategy |
|---|---|---|
| ~260+ | ~80–90% | IM-focused |
| ~250 | ~65–80% | IM-focused, FM backup |
| ~240 | ~45–60% (60–70% with FM) | IM + FM in parallel |
| <235 | ~25–40% | FM-heavy |

Observership-only exposure or graduation 4–5 years back lowers each band ~10–15 points; any USMLE fail lowers it substantially.

## Common mistakes

- Applying to too few programs — matched IMGs ranked ~2.5× as many [NRMP 2024](https://www.nrmp.org/wp-content/uploads/2024/09/Charting_Outcomes_IMG2024.pdf).
- Adding a parallel specialty without specialty-specific letters [NRMP 2026](https://www.nrmp.org/wp-content/uploads/2026/08/2026-PD-Survey-Report-Narrative-FINAL.pdf).
- Ignoring year-of-graduation cutoffs [FREIDA](https://freida.ama-assn.org/program/1400531051).
- Chasing reach fields where non-US IMGs hold <8% of seats [NRMP 2026](https://www.nrmp.org/wp-content/uploads/2026/05/Main_Match_Results_and_Data-2026.pdf).
