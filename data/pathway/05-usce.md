# 05. US clinical exposure, letters and the B-1 visa

*Stage key `usce` · last verified 2026-11-15 · re-verify quarterly and immediately on policy news.*

MBBS graduates can generally do only observerships (shadowing, no patient contact); hands-on clerkships on a B-1 are limited to students [AMA](https://ama-assn.org/life-career/observership-program-listings-international-medical-graduates) [Temple ISSS](https://global.temple.edu/isss/faculty-staff-and-researchers/temple-links/medical-activities). The main value is the specialty-specific US letter, cited by 84–86% of program directors for interview selection [NRMP 2024](https://www.nrmp.org/wp-content/uploads/2024/08/2024-PD-Survey-Report-narrative_Final.pdf) [NRMP 2026](https://www.nrmp.org/wp-content/uploads/2026/08/2026-PD-Survey-Report-Narrative-FINAL.pdf). B1/B2 interview waits in India were about 6–12 months in Sept 2026 [TravelManToday](https://travelmantoday.com/us-visa-wait-times-indians-2026/), and some hosts require the B-1 before you apply [UTHSCSA](https://lsom.uthscsa.edu/hospital-medicine/home/hospital-medicine-observerships/), so the visa is the hidden critical path.

## Task details

**Understand what an MBBS graduate can do** (`usce-01`) — International graduates can apply only for observerships, which are strictly shadowing with no hands-on patient contact [AMA](https://ama-assn.org/life-career/observership-program-listings-international-medical-graduates). The B-1 for unpaid hands-on clerkships is limited to medical students, and observers generally may not examine patients, touch the EHR or write notes [Temple ISSS citing 9 FAM](https://global.temple.edu/isss/faculty-staff-and-researchers/temple-links/medical-activities).

**File a DS-160 and book the B1/B2 interview now** (`usce-02`) — B1/B2 waits (State Dept data via a secondary source, 17 Sep 2026) were about 6 months in Kolkata, 7 in Chennai, 7.5 in New Delhi, 11 in Mumbai and 11.5 in Hyderabad [TravelManToday](https://travelmantoday.com/us-visa-wait-times-indians-2026/); check the live page [State Dept](https://travel.state.gov/content/travel/en/us-visas/visa-information-resources/global-visa-wait-times.html). The B1/B2 fee is $185 [visavisa.in](https://visavisa.in/us-visa/fees). You may book at any Indian post, because since 6 Sep 2025 you must interview in your country of nationality or residence [Faegre Drinker](https://www.faegredrinker.com/en/insights/publications/2025/9/new-home-country-only-visa-interview-rule-and-termination-of-the-interview-waiver).

**Plan for an in-person interview** (`usce-03`) — From 2 Sep 2025 most applicants must interview in person; the dropbox waiver remains only for narrow B1/B2 renewals (prior visa expired within 12 months, same class, no refusals) [Faegre Drinker](https://www.faegredrinker.com/en/insights/publications/2025/9/new-home-country-only-visa-interview-rule-and-termination-of-the-interview-waiver). A $250 Visa Integrity Fee was legislated in 2025 but no official notice of collection at Indian posts had been found as of 23 Oct 2026 (verify) [visavisa.in](https://visavisa.in/us-visa/fees) [Boundless](https://www.boundless.com/blog/visa-integrity-fee).

**Keep every visa application truthful and consistent** (`usce-04`) — In 2026 some Indian physicians were refused visa appointments; Intealth briefed US agencies that B-visa observerships and J-1 residency are separate activities [AAIM](https://www.im.org/about/alliance-news/article/2026/06/11/intealth-shares-update-on-visa-appointments-for-physicians-from-india). Be ready at the port of entry to explain that the visit is an unpaid observership; B status allows a stay of up to 6 months [Temple ISSS](https://global.temple.edu/isss/faculty-staff-and-researchers/temple-links/medical-activities). Keep public social-media profiles consistent with your DS-160.

**Send the KU Medical Center inquiry before 1 Dec 2026** (`usce-05`) — KUMC accepts IMGs regardless of immigration status (students are ineligible), charges a $50 inquiry fee per program and $3,000 a month, for up to 4 weeks [KUMC](https://www.kumc.edu/academic-and-student-affairs/departments/office-of-international-programs/inbound-programs/information-for-irsd-observers-and-visitors/international-observership-program.html). Jan–Jun 2027 placements: apply 15 Oct–1 Dec 2026; Jul–Dec 2027 placements: apply 15 Jan–15 Mar 2027 [KUMC](https://www.kumc.edu/academic-and-student-affairs/departments/office-of-international-programs/inbound-programs/information-for-irsd-observers-and-visitors/international-observership-program.html).

**Apply to UAB once Step 1 is passed** (`usce-06`) — UAB requires an MBBS within the last 5 years and a passed Step 1; it charges a $350 non-refundable application fee plus $4,250 per 4 weeks once a host is found [UAB](https://www.uab.edu/medicine/international/international-programs/observership). Apply 3 months before the start date; you stay in the candidate pool for up to 6 months, and UAB recommends a B-1/B-2 with a B-1 admission stamp [UAB](https://www.uab.edu/medicine/international/international-programs/observership).

**Apply to UT Health San Antonio Hospital Medicine (needs B-1 first)** (`usce-07`) — Requires intent to apply for US residency, a passed Step 1 and an LOR or dean's letter; costs $50 + $450 processing + $500 a week (about $2,450 for 4 weeks) plus a $10 badge [UTHSCSA](https://lsom.uthscsa.edu/hospital-medicine/home/hospital-medicine-observerships/). A B-1 is required before you apply and B-2 admission is not acceptable; apply at least 60 days ahead (current windows Feb–Apr 2027) [UTHSCSA](https://lsom.uthscsa.edu/hospital-medicine/home/hospital-medicine-observerships/).

**Consider Missouri Neurology only with real neurology interest** (`usce-08`) — Requires Step 1 and Step 2 CK, an MU neurology sponsor and 3 LORs; $750 application + $1,800 per 4-week block, up to three blocks, no July starts [University of Missouri](https://www.medicine.missouri.edu/departments/neurology/observership).

**SUNY Upstate Medicine: only after ECFMG certification** (`usce-09`) — Requires first-attempt scores of 200 or more on Step 1 and Step 2 CK and a valid ECFMG certificate; $300 application + $1,500 per 2-week rotation, no July starts [SUNY Upstate](https://upstate.edu/medicine/observership). Submit a complete file 8 weeks before the start; a decision comes within 4 weeks [SUNY Upstate](https://upstate.edu/medicine/observership).

**Know the expensive and high-bar options** (`usce-10`) — Johns Hopkins Hospital Medicine is built for IMGs: $150 application + $9,000 (Oct–Apr) or $10,000 (May–Sep) for 6 weeks, and observers may request a letter on completion [Hopkins](https://hopkinsmedicine.org/hospital-medicine/medical-education-programs/hospital-medicine-observership-program). Mount Sinai Medical Center Miami Beach (not Mount Sinai New York) requires USMLE 220 or more on every Step taken and applications 90 days ahead [MSMC](https://www.msmc.com/education/educational-courses-and-events/international-observership-courses/).

**Skip programs that will not help your Match** (`usce-11`) — UCLA Health charges $750, requires a B-1, and is “unable to provide any recommendation letters” [UCLA](https://www.uclahealth.org/international-services/consulting-education-services/medical-education-training/physicians/physician-observerships). Cleveland Clinic will not consider applicants who intend to apply for US residency and does not refund fees [Cleveland Clinic](https://my.clevelandclinic.org/departments/international-medical-education/physician-programs/physician-observer). Keck USC requires current employment by a foreign health organization [USC](https://sites.usc.edu/healthcare-edu/observership).

**Ask about the letter policy in writing before paying** (`usce-12`) — Specialty-specific letters are cited by 84% of program directors for interview selection [NRMP 2024](https://www.nrmp.org/wp-content/uploads/2024/08/2024-PD-Survey-Report-narrative_Final.pdf). Hosts differ: Hopkins writes letters on request [Hopkins](https://hopkinsmedicine.org/hospital-medicine/medical-education-programs/hospital-medicine-observership-program), UCLA writes none [UCLA](https://www.uclahealth.org/international-services/consulting-education-services/medical-education-training/physicians/physician-observerships). Choose 4-week blocks with one consistent attending, because 2-week rotations rarely produce a detailed letter.

**Check program-specific USCE rules for your target list** (`usce-13`) — UCSF-Fresno IM requires IMGs without US rotations to have at least 4 weeks of observership in at least 2 clinical settings, plus 3 LORs including one from a Department of Medicine faculty member [UCSF Fresno](https://fresno.ucsf.edu/internal-medicine/applicants). Check each shortlisted program's page for similar rules.

**Screen paid brokers for red flags** (`usce-14`) — Brokers such as AMOpportunities hide prices until you create an account [AMO](https://www.amopportunities.org/students/international-medical-graduates), while AMA guidance says observerships are not intended to be organized for profit [AMA](https://www.ama-assn.org/education/international-medical-education/guidelines-establishing-observership-international). “Hands-on” work offered to a graduate on a B visa conflicts with the student-only clerkship rule [Temple ISSS](https://global.temple.edu/isss/faculty-staff-and-researchers/temple-links/medical-activities). Other red flags: guaranteed letters, unnamed preceptors, payment into personal accounts, no written refund policy.

**Treat virtual or telehealth rotations as filler** (`usce-15`) — Virtual rotations are sold by brokers [AMO](https://amopportunities.org/virtual-rotations), but no program-director survey data shows they are valued. Do not let them replace in-person observership time.

**Do 4–8 weeks of observership in May–August** (`usce-16`) — Plan blocks so letters can be uploaded by September; Missouri and SUNY Upstate do not start blocks in July [Missouri](https://www.medicine.missouri.edu/departments/neurology/observership) [Upstate](https://upstate.edu/medicine/observership). Present cases verbally where allowed, read on your patients, and ask for mid-rotation feedback.

**Ask for a strong, specific letter near the end of each block** (`usce-17`) — Specialty-specific letters were used by 86% of programs for interview selection in 2026 [NRMP 2026](https://www.nrmp.org/wp-content/uploads/2026/08/2026-PD-Survey-Report-Narrative-FINAL.pdf). Give the attending your CV and highlights; letters are uploaded by the writer through ERAS, not by you [AAMC ERAS](https://www.aamc.org/services/eras-for-institutions/medical-schools/timeline). Indian faculty letters can fill remaining slots.

**If offered a US research post, check the visa first** (`usce-18`) — J-1 research-scholar status is a different category from the ECFMG clinical J-1 [Mayo Clinic](https://college.mayo.edu/admissions-and-tuition/immigration-and-visas/j-1-visa/j-1-ecfmg-scholars/). It may carry 212(e) and a 24-month bar on repeat research-scholar status (inference, verify with an immigration attorney before accepting).

## Common mistakes

- Waiting to book the B1/B2 interview until an observership is confirmed — waits were 6–12 months [TravelManToday](https://travelmantoday.com/us-visa-wait-times-indians-2026/).
- Paying UCLA or Cleveland Clinic for Match purposes — no letters / excludes residency-seekers [UCLA](https://www.uclahealth.org/international-services/consulting-education-services/medical-education-training/physicians/physician-observerships) [Cleveland Clinic](https://my.clevelandclinic.org/departments/international-medical-education/physician-programs/physician-observer).
- Accepting “hands-on” work as a graduate on a B visa [Temple ISSS](https://global.temple.edu/isss/faculty-staff-and-researchers/temple-links/medical-activities).
- Booking 2-week blocks with rotating attendings, which rarely produce a detailed letter.
- Applying to SUNY Upstate before you hold the ECFMG certificate [Upstate](https://upstate.edu/medicine/observership).
- Inconsistent visa applications or social media [AAIM](https://www.im.org/about/alliance-news/article/2026/06/11/intealth-shares-update-on-visa-appointments-for-physicians-from-india).
