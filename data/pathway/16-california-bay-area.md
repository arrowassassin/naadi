# 16. California and the Bay Area

*Stage key `california_bay_area` · last verified 2026-11-15 · re-verify quarterly and immediately on policy news.*

Bay Area academic and Kaiser programs take almost no non-US IMGs (Kaiser NorCal Oakland and SF IM about 0%, Santa Clara Valley about 3%) [ResidencyMatch.ai](https://www.residencymatch.ai/img-friendly/by-state/ca). Realistic California targets are Central Valley and Inland Empire programs, nearly all J-1 only; Highland in Oakland is a stretch (577 interviews for 30 seats) [FREIDA](https://freida.ama-assn.org/program/1400531051). California's Conrad 30 requires shortage-area practice [HCAI](https://hcai.ca.gov/workforce-capacity/california-primary-care-office/j-1-visa-waiver-program/), so a Bay Area job is more realistic as the second job after a waiver, not the first.

## Task details

**Build a realistic California residency list** (`ca-01`) — High-IMG California IM programs (nearly all J-1) include San Joaquin General, Adventist Health Tulare, Prime West sites, St Agnes Fresno, Kern Medical, UCR/St Bernardine and UCSF-Fresno [ResidencyMatch.ai, secondary](https://www.residencymatch.ai/img-friendly/by-state/ca). Highland in Oakland is a stretch: 577 interviews for 30 PGY-1 seats [FREIDA](https://freida.ama-assn.org/program/1400531051).

**Treat Bay Area academic and Kaiser programs as reaches** (`ca-02`) — Kaiser NorCal Oakland and San Francisco IM show about 0% IMGs and Santa Clara Valley about 3% [ResidencyMatch.ai](https://www.residencymatch.ai/img-friendly/by-state/ca). SCVMC's IM application page lists no visa, graduation-year or score information [SCVMC](https://scvmc.scvh.org/education-training/programs/internal-medicine-residency/application-process).

**Check Kern and UCSF-Fresno requirements** (`ca-03`) — Kern Medical IM is J-1 only, needs the ECFMG certificate and Steps 1–2, and asks for ERAS signals instead of emailed letters of interest [Kern](https://www.kernmedical.com/academics/residency-fellowship-programs/internal-medicine/international-residents/). UCSF-Fresno IM is J-1 only and considers IMGs less than 5 years from graduation [UCSF Fresno](https://fresno.ucsf.edu/internal-medicine/applicants).

**Network through Stanford's visiting-observer programs** (`ca-04`) — Stanford OB/GYN accepts international physicians for up to 30 days in 12 months with approval taking up to 6 weeks; no visa or housing is provided [Stanford](https://obgyn.stanford.edu/education/visiting-observer.html). Stanford Health Care Tri-Valley requires a host with privileges there, filing at least 3 weeks ahead [SHC Tri-Valley](https://med.stanford.edu/shctv/education/observership.html). Fees and letter policies are unpublished.

**Compare Bay Area resident pay** (`ca-05`) — 2026–27 PGY-1: UCSF $101,801 [UCSF](https://meded.ucsf.edu/gme-resident-and-fellow-resource-hub/salary-and-benefits); Stanford $96,948.80 (pending contract) [Stanford](https://med.stanford.edu/gme/housestaff/current/stipends.html); Kaiser NorCal Oakland IM $97,939 [FREIDA](https://freida.ama-assn.org/program/1400512052/features-benefits); Highland IM $83,905 [FREIDA](https://freida.ama-assn.org/program/1400531051/features-benefits). All are well above the AAMC Western PGY-1 average of $77,649 [AMA](https://www.ama-assn.org/medical-residents/residency-life/resident-physician-pay-still-rising-growth-trails-inflation).

**Check rents against HUD Fair Market Rents** (`ca-06`) — FY2027 1BR FMRs: San Francisco $3,003, San Jose $2,778, Oakland–Fremont $2,290, Stockton–Lodi $1,628, Fresno $1,363 (secondary transcription; verify at huduser.gov) [StartBusinessByState](https://startbusinessbystate.com/?p=20015). At the Oakland FMR a PGY-1 on $97–102k spends about 27% of gross pay on rent (inference).

**Get your California PTL on time** (`ca-07`) — California IMG residents need a PTL within 180 days of starting, and can get the full license after 24 months of approved training, with Step 3 required [MBC](https://www.mbc.ca.gov/Licensing/Physicians-and-Surgeons/Apply/). Kern requires residents to meet California licensure requirements with no stipulations on Medical Board status [Kern](https://www.kernmedical.com/academics/residency-fellowship-programs/internal-medicine/international-residents/).

**Know that training outside California still works** (`ca-08`) — Residency in any ACGME-accredited program counts toward the California training requirement; California's license is a separate application you can start in your final year (inference from MBC rules) [MBC](https://www.mbc.ca.gov/Licensing/Physicians-and-Surgeons/Apply/). Do not let the Bay Area goal shrink your residency list.

**Target Bay-adjacent shortage-area sites for the waiver** (`ca-09`) — California's Conrad 30 requires full-time practice in a HPSA, MUA or MUP and gives rural sites priority [HCAI](https://hcai.ca.gov/workforce-capacity/california-primary-care-office/j-1-visa-waiver-program/). Most Bay Area urban hospitals will not qualify; a Bay Area FQHC in a HPSA is possible but not assured (inference).

**Do not wait for a California alternative pathway** (`ca-10`) — AB 2386, a provisional license for internationally licensed physicians, passed the Assembly but was held in Senate Appropriations on 13 Aug 2026 and never reached the governor [CalMatters](https://calmatters.digitaldemocracy.org/bills/ca_202520260ab2386). The Mexico physicians program re-established by AB 2860 is Mexico-specific [FSMB](https://inte.fsmb.org/siteassets/advocacy/policies/states-with-enacted-and-proposed-additional-img-licensure-pathways-key-issue-chart.pdf).

**Get your California driver license after you settle** (`ca-11`) — California says all residents need a California license; applicants need an original legal-presence document valid at least 30 days to start and 60 days to receive a photo card [CA DMV](https://www.dmv.ca.gov/portal/driver-licenses-identification-cards/driver-licenses-dl/) [CA DMV](https://www.dmv.ca.gov/portal/driver-licenses-identification-cards/limited-term-for-legal-presence/). An SSN is required only if you are authorized to work, which a J-1 resident is.

## Best / typical / worst scenarios

| Scenario | Path | Bay Area practice |
|---|---|---|
| **Best** | Match 2028 on J-1; visa on time; 3-year IM residency to June 2031; California Conrad 30 slot at a Bay-adjacent shortage-area site or FQHC [HCAI](https://hcai.ca.gov/workforce-capacity/california-primary-care-office/j-1-visa-waiver-program/); fee-exempt J-1→H-1B change of status [FSMB/attorneys](https://www.fsmb.org/siteassets/baw-speaker-presentation/session-8-imgs-fsmb-attorneys-nov-2025.pdf); early I-140 locks a priority date. | Bay Area-adjacent by 2031; free choice of job after about 2034 |
| **Typical** | J-1; waiver job in the Central Valley, a rural area or another state 2031–34; then H-1B. EB-2 India backlog [F1Jobs](https://www.f1jobs.io/resources/blog/november-2026-visa-bulletin-explained-eb2-eb3-india). | Moves to the Bay Area after about 2034; green card in the late 2030s or later |
| **Worst** | Unmatched in 2028 and needs a second cycle; or J-1 interview delayed past 1 July [The Cancer Letter](https://cancerletter.com/cancer-policy/20260702_5c/); or no waiver job, forcing two years back in India [ECFMG](https://ecfmg.org/evsp/applying-general.html). | 2037 or later, or never |

*Scenarios are Naadi synthesis from the cited rules, not predictions.*

## Common mistakes

- Building the residency list around Bay Area programs that take almost no non-US IMGs.
- Assuming a Bay Area urban hospital qualifies for Conrad 30 [HCAI](https://hcai.ca.gov/workforce-capacity/california-primary-care-office/j-1-visa-waiver-program/).
- Treating HUD FMRs as listing prices — verify and add a margin [HUD via StartBusinessByState](https://startbusinessbystate.com/?p=20015).
