# 07. ERAS application

*Stage key `eras_application` · last verified 2026-11-15 · re-verify quarterly and immediately on policy news.*

ERAS 2028 dates are not yet published; in the 2027 cycle MyERAS opened 4 Jun 2026, applicants could submit from 2 Sep and programs reviewed from 23 Sep [AAMC](https://www.aamc.org/services/eras-for-institutions/medical-schools/timeline). Fees are $11 per program for the first 30 and $30 each after, per specialty [Lecturio](https://www.lecturio.com/blog/eras-2027-timeline-residency-application-strategy/). Internal Medicine gives 3 gold and 12 silver signals [AAMC](https://students-residents.aamc.org/applying-residencies-eras/program-signaling-2027-myeras-application-season). Submit on day one with 100–150 IM and about 30 FM programs.

## Task details

**Note when MyERAS opens** (`eras-01`) — The 2027 ERAS season opened 4 Jun 2026; 2028 dates are not yet published, so early June 2027 is inferred [AAMC](https://www.aamc.org/services/eras-for-institutions/medical-schools/timeline). Create your MyERAS account and start the application in the first week.

**Buy your ERAS token from ECFMG** (`eras-02`) — The ERAS token is listed at $165 [IMGPrep](https://www.imgprep.com/ecfmg-certification-cost); some guides say $185 (verify). ECFMG released 2027 tokens on 24 Jun 2026 [Lecturio, secondary](https://www.lecturio.com/blog/eras-2027-timeline-residency-application-strategy/).

**Request USMLE transcripts through FSMB** (`eras-03`) — USMLE transcripts for ERAS are reported to come via FSMB from June 2026 [Lecturio, secondary](https://www.lecturio.com/blog/eras-2027-timeline-residency-application-strategy/). Request them as soon as MyERAS is open.

**Build a 100–150 IM and ~30 FM program list** (`eras-04`) — Verify each program's J-1 policy and graduation-year rule in FREIDA, e.g. Highland IM is J-1 only with a 5-year limit [FREIDA](https://freida.ama-assn.org/program/1400531051). Naadi analysis: most IMG interviews come from IMG-heavy states such as NY, NJ, MI, OH, PA, IL and TX.

**Budget ERAS application fees** (`eras-05`) — ERAS charges $11 per program for the first 30 and $30 each from the 31st, resetting per specialty [Lecturio](https://www.lecturio.com/blog/eras-2027-timeline-residency-application-strategy/). 100 IM programs = $2,430; 150 = $3,930; add about 30 FM programs at $330.

**Plan your program signals** (`eras-06`) — 2027 signals: IM 3 gold + 12 silver; FM 5; Pediatrics 5; Psychiatry 10; Neurology 8; Pathology 5 [AAMC](https://students-residents.aamc.org/applying-residencies-eras/program-signaling-2027-myeras-application-season). Some programs ask for signals instead of letters of interest, e.g. Kern Medical IM [Kern](https://www.kernmedical.com/academics/residency-fellowship-programs/internal-medicine/international-residents/). Spend gold signals on realistic IMG-friendly programs, not UCSF or Stanford.

**Write the personal statement; buy at most one review** (`eras-07`) — Keep paid help to one personal-statement review and 2–3 mock interviews with a written scope and refund policy. Match A Resident has operated since 2004 and been BBB-accredited since 2011 but has a no-refund-after-access policy [BBB](https://prod.bbb.org/us/ca/san-diego/profile/innovation/residency-prep-llc-1126-172000119) and mixed reviews [SDN](https://forums.studentdoctor.net/threads/match-a-resident.1417738).

**Confirm all letters are uploaded** (`eras-08`) — Specialty-specific letters were used by 86% of programs for interview selection in 2026 [NRMP 2026](https://www.nrmp.org/wp-content/uploads/2026/08/2026-PD-Survey-Report-Narrative-FINAL.pdf). Check MyERAS that each US letter writer has uploaded before you submit.

**Submit on day one** (`eras-09`) — In the 2027 cycle applicants could submit from 2 Sep 2026 and programs began reviewing on 23 Sep [AAMC](https://www.aamc.org/services/eras-for-institutions/medical-schools/timeline); 2028 dates (about 1–2 Sep 2027) are inferred. IMG applications sent after review opens lose ground.

**Register for the NRMP Main Match** (`eras-10`) — For the 2027 Match, registration opened 15 Sep 2026 and the standard deadline is 29 Jan 2027, with a $50 late fee after [NRMP](https://www.nrmp.org/match-calendars/main-residency-applicants/). The base registration fee was not captured in the sources (verify on NRMP).

## Common mistakes

- Submitting after programs start reviewing (2027 cycle: 23 Sep) [AAMC](https://www.aamc.org/services/eras-for-institutions/medical-schools/timeline).
- Spending gold signals on UCSF or Stanford.
- Not checking J-1 and graduation-year rules per program in FREIDA [FREIDA](https://freida.ama-assn.org/program/1400531051).
- Buying a bundled consulting package with no refund policy [BBB](https://prod.bbb.org/us/ca/san-diego/profile/innovation/residency-prep-llc-1126-172000119).
