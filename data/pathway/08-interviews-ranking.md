*Stage key `interviews_ranking` · last verified 2026-11-15 · re-verify quarterly and immediately on policy news.*

Interviews run roughly October to January [Lecturio](https://www.lecturio.com/blog/eras-2027-timeline-residency-application-strategy/). Program directors' top ranking factors are interpersonal skills (89%) and interview interactions (87%) [NRMP 2024 PD Survey](https://www.nrmp.org/wp-content/uploads/2024/08/2024-PD-Survey-Report-narrative_Final.pdf). ## Task details

**Prepare for an October–January interview season** (`intv-01`) — Interviews typically run October to January [Lecturio](https://www.lecturio.com/blog/eras-2027-timeline-residency-application-strategy/); most IM programs have remained largely virtual (verify). Set up a quiet room, reliable internet and a neutral background before invitations arrive.

**Do 2–3 mock interviews** (`intv-02`) — Program directors rate interpersonal skills (89%, mean 4.8/5) and interview interactions (87%, 4.8) as top ranking factors [NRMP 2024 PD Survey](https://www.nrmp.org/wp-content/uploads/2024/08/2024-PD-Survey-Report-narrative_Final.pdf). Practise explaining your observership cases and why you want this specialty.

**Track invitations and aim for six or more ranks** (`intv-03`) — Matched non-US IMGs in IM ranked a mean of 6.1 contiguous programs vs 2.5 for unmatched (2024) [NRMP 2024](https://www.nrmp.org/wp-content/uploads/2024/09/Charting_Outcomes_IMG2024.pdf). Log every invitation in Naadi so you can see early whether you need to widen your list.

**Ask each program which visas it sponsors** (`intv-04`) — Programs differ: Highland IM sponsors J-1 only [FREIDA](https://freida.ama-assn.org/program/1400531051/features-benefits), while Kaiser NorCal Oakland IM sponsors J-1 and H-1B [FREIDA](https://freida.ama-assn.org/program/1400512052/features-benefits). Confirm in writing before ranking.

**Make sure both Step passes are in by the ROL deadline** (`intv-05`) — IMGs must pass Step 1 and Step 2 CK by the NRMP Rank Order List certification deadline [ECFMG](https://apps.wdoms.org/certification-pathways/). You also need an accepted, unexpired Pathway at that deadline [ECFMG](https://corp.ecfmg.org/certification-pathways/expiration.html).

**Rank every program you would attend** (`intv-06`) — Matched IMGs ranked about 2.5 times as many programs as unmatched IMGs [NRMP 2024](https://www.nrmp.org/wp-content/uploads/2024/09/Charting_Outcomes_IMG2024.pdf). Rank in true preference order, and leave off only programs you would not attend.

**Certify your Rank Order List** (`intv-07`) — For the 2027 Match, ranking opens 1 Feb 2027 and the deadline is 3 Mar 2027 at 9 PM EST [NRMP](https://www.nrmp.org/match-calendars/main-residency-applicants/); the 2028 deadline (about early March) is inferred. Certify a few days early.

## Common mistakes

- Ranking too few programs [NRMP 2024](https://www.nrmp.org/wp-content/uploads/2024/09/Charting_Outcomes_IMG2024.pdf).
- Ranking an H-1B-only program without confirming the fee and Step 3 position [FSMB/attorneys](https://www.fsmb.org/siteassets/baw-speaker-presentation/session-8-imgs-fsmb-attorneys-nov-2025.pdf).
- Missing that both Step passes and an unexpired Pathway must exist by the ROL deadline [ECFMG](https://apps.wdoms.org/certification-pathways/) [ECFMG](https://corp.ecfmg.org/certification-pathways/expiration.html).
