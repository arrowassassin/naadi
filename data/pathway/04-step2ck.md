# 04. USMLE Step 2 CK

*Stage key `step2ck` · last verified 2026-11-15 · re-verify quarterly and immediately on policy news.*

Step 2 CK is scored; the pass mark is 218 since 1 Jul 2025 and the mean is about 250 [iatroX](https://www.iatrox.com/blog/usmle-step-2-ck-2026-passing-standard-mean-score-you-need). This is the score programs filter on: 89% of programs use it for interview decisions [NRMP 2026 PD Survey](https://www.nrmp.org/wp-content/uploads/2026/08/2026-PD-Survey-Report-Narrative-FINAL.pdf). Matched non-US IMGs in Internal Medicine averaged 248 vs 242 for unmatched (2024) [NRMP](https://www.nrmp.org/wp-content/uploads/2024/09/Charting_Outcomes_IMG2024.pdf). Budget about 12 weeks, sit only when predictors are in range, and remember a passed Step cannot be retaken for a higher score [USMLE](https://www.usmle.org/bulletin-information/eligibility).

## Task details

**Apply for Step 2 CK in the FSMB portal** (`s2ck-01`) — The 2026 fee in India is $695 plus a $235 international surcharge, about ₹82,800 before any GST [IMGPrep](https://www.imgprep.com/ecfmg-certification-cost). Apply as soon as Step 1 is done so the permit is ready for your 12-week block.

**Book the Prometric seat** (`s2ck-02`) — Step 2 CK uses the same Indian Prometric centres as Step 1 [Careers360](https://studyabroad.careers360.com/articles/usmle-test-centres). Book early in the eligibility period and keep a fallback city.

**Buy UWorld Step 2 CK** (`s2ck-03`) — UWorld CK costs $459 for 90 days or $519 for 180 days (about ₹41–46k); 180-day plans add a one-time reset [UWorld](https://medical.uworld.com/usmle/usmle-step-2-ck/). This is the core question bank for CK.

**Weeks 1–2: baseline CCSSA and US conventions** (`s2ck-04`) — Take a baseline NBME CCSSA (sold via MyNBME) [NBME](https://www.nbme.org/examinees/self-assessments). Spend these weeks learning US management conventions: screening, consent, ethics and patient safety, which differ from Indian practice.

**Weeks 3–8: 80–120 timed random questions a day** (`s2ck-05`) — Work UWorld CK timed and random [UWorld](https://medical.uworld.com/usmle/usmle-step-2-ck/), use Divine Intervention [Divine](https://divineinterventionpodcasts.com/category/usmle-step-1/organ-systems-usmle-step-1/) and Mehlman CK PDFs [TestPrepPal](https://testpreppal.com/usmle/cheat-sheets/mehlman-medical) for weak areas, and Anki for misses. Take a CCSSA about every 2 weeks.

**Weeks 9–11: finish UWorld and take UWSA 1 and 2** (`s2ck-06`) — UWorld self-assessments cost $50 each or $120 for the bundle [UWorld](https://medical.uworld.com/usmle/usmle-step-2-ck/). Aim for 245–250+ on predictors: matched non-US IMGs in IM averaged 248 vs 242 for unmatched in 2024 [NRMP](https://www.nrmp.org/wp-content/uploads/2024/09/Charting_Outcomes_IMG2024.pdf).

**Know the format and pass mark** (`s2ck-07`) — The pass mark is 218 for tests on or after 1 Jul 2025, and the mean is about 250 (SD 15) [iatroX, secondary](https://www.iatrox.com/blog/usmle-step-2-ck-2026-passing-standard-mean-score-you-need). From 7 May 2026 the exam is reported as 16 × 30-minute blocks, 316 items, in a 9-hour day [Lecturio, secondary](https://www.lecturio.com/blog/2026-usmle-changes-step-1-2-ck-software-block-updates/).

**Week 12: Free 120, a full-length day, weak-topic repair** (`s2ck-08`) — Use USMLE's free sample questions [USMLE](https://www.usmle.org/prepare-your-exam) and do one full-length stamina day at the 9-hour length. Sit only if predictors are in range; otherwise push 2–4 weeks.

**Go / no-go: do not rush the score that matters** (`s2ck-09`) — A passed Step cannot be retaken for a higher score [USMLE](https://www.usmle.org/bulletin-information/eligibility), and 89% of programs use the CK score for interview decisions [NRMP 2026](https://www.nrmp.org/wp-content/uploads/2026/08/2026-PD-Survey-Report-Narrative-FINAL.pdf). Of programs that require Step 2 CK, 32% set a target score for IMGs [NRMP 2026](https://www.nrmp.org/wp-content/uploads/2026/08/2026-PD-Survey-Report-Narrative-FINAL.pdf).

**Track the result and ECFMG certification** (`s2ck-10`) — The result is expected about 3–4 weeks after the exam (unverified). ECFMG issues the certificate about two weeks after all requirements are met [ECFMG FAQ](https://ecfmg.org/certification-pathways/faqs.html). The IMG first-time Step 2 CK pass rate was 90% in 2024–25 [USMLE](https://www.usmle.org/performance-data).

## 12-week Step 2 CK plan

| Weeks | Focus | Checkpoints |
|---|---|---|
| 1–2 | Baseline CCSSA; UWorld CK by system; US management conventions | Baseline score |
| 3–8 | 80–120 UWorld CK Qs/day, timed random; Divine + Mehlman CK for weak areas; Anki for misses | CCSSA every ~2 weeks |
| 9–11 | Finish UWorld; UWSA 1 and 2; remaining CCSSAs | Aim **245–250+** on predictors |
| 12 | Free 120, full-length day, weak-topic repair | Sit only if in range; else push 2–4 weeks |

Benchmarks: matched non-US IMGs in IM averaged 248 (2024) [NRMP](https://www.nrmp.org/wp-content/uploads/2024/09/Charting_Outcomes_IMG2024.pdf); a 2026 median of 251 is quoted elsewhere but not independently verified.

## Common mistakes

- Rushing CK the way Step 1 was rushed — the score is used by 89% of programs for interviews [NRMP 2026](https://www.nrmp.org/wp-content/uploads/2026/08/2026-PD-Survey-Report-Narrative-FINAL.pdf).
- Studying Indian management protocols instead of US conventions (screening, consent, ethics, safety).
- Sitting with predictors below target — a passed Step cannot be retaken [USMLE](https://www.usmle.org/bulletin-information/eligibility).
- Forgetting the 9-hour, 16-block test day [Lecturio](https://www.lecturio.com/blog/2026-usmle-changes-step-1-2-ck-software-block-updates/).
