# 00. Pathway overview: budgets, master timeline, policy watch

*Cross-cutting reference for all stages · last verified 2026-11-15.*

The route from an Indian MBBS to US residency is a parallel race, not a staged journey: exams, ECFMG certification, US clinical exposure with letters, and visas must converge by the September ERAS submission. A slip in any one pushes the Match back a year.

## Master timeline (2028 Match target)

Target for a 2028 Match (dates marked *inferred* follow past cycles; ERAS 2028 is unpublished [AAMC](https://www.aamc.org/services/eras-for-institutions/medical-schools/timeline)).

|---|---|
| 16 Nov 2026 | Start Step 1 plan; open MyIntealth; book OET and the B1 interview |
| 19 Dec 2026 | OET Medicine (paper); results ~5 Jan 2027 [TalkDrill](https://www.talkdrill.com/blog/exams/oet/exam-dates-2026/) |
| Mid/late Jan 2027 | Step 1 (only if two fresh NBMEs ~65–70+) |
| By 31 Jan 2027 | 2027 Pathway application [ECFMG](https://apps.wdoms.org/certification-pathways/); Pathway 3 electronic attestation by 15 Feb [ECFMG](https://certification.ecfmg.org/certification-pathways/pathways-3-5.html) |
| Feb–May 2027 | Step 2 CK block (~12 weeks); apply to observerships |
| ~May 2027 | Step 2 CK |
| May–Aug 2027 | 4–8 weeks of observership; 2–3 US letters |
| ~Jun–Jul 2027 | ECFMG certificate once Step 1, Step 2 CK and Pathway are done |

| ~1–2 Sep 2027 (*inferred*) | Submit ERAS on day one |
| Oct 2027–Jan 2028 | Interviews |

| Mar–Jun 2028 | MoHFW SoN, Intealth J-1, J-1 interview in India |

|---|---|---|---|
| **H-1B $100,000 fee (Proclamation 10973)** | Applies to new petitions for physicians outside the US; not to J-1→H-1B change of status [FSMB/attorneys](https://www.fsmb.org/siteassets/baw-speaker-presentation/session-8-imgs-fsmb-attorneys-nov-2025.pdf). Vacated 8 Jun 2026; First Circuit denied a stay 24 Jul [Clark Hill](https://www.clarkhill.com/news-events/news/first-circuit-blocks-reinstatement-of-100k-h-1b-fee/). Renewed 18 Sep 2026 to run to 21 Sep 2027 [Klasko](https://www.klaskolaw.com/h-1b-100000-fee-extended-one-year/). Renewal vacated and enjoined 30 Sep 2026 (N.D. Cal.) [Forbes](https://www.forbes.com/sites/stuartanderson/2026/10/01/judge-strikes-down-latest-version-of-100000-h-1b-immigration-fee/). **Not being collected, but litigation continues**; a proposed $103,265 fee rule (Aug 2026) is pending [Forbes](https://www.forbes.com/sites/stuartanderson/2026/10/01/judge-strikes-down-latest-version-of-100000-h-1b-immigration-fee/). A physician-exemption bill had no hearing as of May 2026 [AAPPR](https://aappr.org/2026/03/23/changes-to-h-1b-process-what-health-recruiters-need-to-know-update-march-23-2026/) [CMA](https://www.cmadocs.org/newsroom/news/view/ArticleId/51159/Bipartisan-bill-would-exempt-physicians-from-100-000-H-1B-visa-filing-fee). | Treat H-1B-from-abroad as a bonus, J-1 as default. Re-check before ranking H-1B programs. | FSMB, Clark Hill, Klasko, Forbes |
| **J-1 fixed admission period (end of D/S)** | DHS final rule published 17 Jul 2026: fixed Admit Until Date (program end + 30 days, max 4 years per admission). Due 15 Sep 2026; **postponed, no new date** as of 23 Sep 2026 [UTHSCSA OIS](https://wp.uthscsa.edu/ois/immigration/ecfmg-j-1/elimination-of-duration-of-status-impact-on-ecfmg-j-1-alien-physicians/) [Klasko](https://www.klaskolaw.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/2026.09.09-Presentation-Duration-of-Status-Eliminated_Navigating-the-New-Fixed-Admission-Period_Healthcare-RG-TD-IW.pdf). | Check the I-94 at every entry; renew the passport early; extensions would need Form I-539 if the rule takes effect. | UTHSCSA OIS, Klasko |
| **MoHFW Statement of Need bond** | Each SoN needs a ₹10 lakh surety bond on ₹100 stamp paper with two sureties; return-to-India clause with ₹10 lakh + 12% interest demand (scan partly illegible, verify) [MoHFW OM 2023](https://mohfw.gov.in/sites/default/files/Revised%20guidelines%20for%20issuance%20of%20SON%2C%20ENC%20and%20NORI_0.pdf). ## Best / typical / worst scenarios

| Scenario | Path | Bay Area practice |
|---|---|---|
| **Best** | Match 2028 on J-1; visa on time; 3-year IM residency to June 2031; California Conrad 30 slot at a Bay-adjacent shortage-area site or FQHC [HCAI](https://hcai.ca.gov/workforce-capacity/california-primary-care-office/j-1-visa-waiver-program/); fee-exempt J-1→H-1B change of status [FSMB/attorneys](https://www.fsmb.org/siteassets/baw-speaker-presentation/session-8-imgs-fsmb-attorneys-nov-2025.pdf); early I-140 locks a priority date. | Bay Area-adjacent by 2031; free choice of job after about 2034 |
| **Typical** | J-1; waiver job in the Central Valley, a rural area or another state 2031–34; then H-1B. EB-2 India backlog [F1Jobs](https://www.f1jobs.io/resources/blog/november-2026-visa-bulletin-explained-eb2-eb3-india). | Moves to the Bay Area after about 2034; green card in the late 2030s or later |
| **Worst** | Unmatched in 2028 and needs a second cycle; or J-1 interview delayed past 1 July [The Cancer Letter](https://cancerletter.com/cancer-policy/20260702_5c/); or no waiver job, forcing two years back in India [ECFMG](https://ecfmg.org/evsp/applying-general.html). | 2037 or later, or never |

*Scenarios are Naadi synthesis from the cited rules, not predictions.*
