# 03. OET Medicine and ECFMG Pathway

*Stage key `oet_pathway` · last verified 2026-11-15 · re-verify quarterly and immediately on policy news.*

ECFMG certification needs an accepted, unexpired Pathway, which includes OET Medicine at 350 in Listening, Reading and Speaking and 300 in Writing in one sitting [ECFMG](https://ecfmg.org/2026ib/clinical-skills-requirement.html) [ECFMG FAQ](https://ecfmg.org/certification-pathways/faqs.html). No USMLE pass is needed to apply for a 2027 Pathway; passes are needed by the Rank Order List deadline of the Match you enter [2027 FAQ](https://apps.wdoms.org/certification-pathways/faqs.html). The 2027 Pathway deadline is 31 Jan 2027 (ET) and ECFMG must receive OET scores directly by then [ECFMG](https://apps.wdoms.org/certification-pathways/). ECFMG has not announced 2028 Pathways [ECFMG News](https://www.ecfmg.org/news/category/pathways/), so a December 2026 OET plus a 2027 Pathway is low-regret insurance.

## Task details

**Book OET Medicine (paper) for 19 Dec 2026** (`oet-01`) — The last December 2026 paper sitting for India is reported as 19 Dec, with bookings closing 25 Nov and results by about 5 Jan 2027 [TalkDrill, secondary](https://www.talkdrill.com/blog/exams/oet/exam-dates-2026/). The fee is AUD 587 (about ₹34–39k, ~$380), including a AUD 7 booking fee [LeapScholar](https://leapscholar.com/blog/?p=86136). ECFMG recommends testing on or before the last scheduled December 2026 date for the 2027 Pathways [2027 FAQ](https://apps.wdoms.org/certification-pathways/faqs.html).

**Enter your MyIntealth ID (not your USMLE ID) on the OET booking** (`oet-02`) — The 2027 FAQ says you must provide your MyIntealth ID to OET so results go to ECFMG, and warns against entering the wrong ID [2027 FAQ](https://apps.wdoms.org/certification-pathways/faqs.html). Create the MyIntealth account before booking.

**Know the score rule: 350/350/350/300 in one sitting** (`oet-03`) — ECFMG requires 350 in Listening, Reading and Speaking and 300 in Writing, all from one administration [ECFMG FAQ](https://ecfmg.org/certification-pathways/faqs.html). Every applicant must take OET regardless of native language, so an English-medium MBBS gives no waiver [ECFMG FAQ](https://ecfmg.org/certification-pathways/faqs.html). A sub-test-only retake (AUD 315/415/515 for 1/2/3 sub-tests [LeapScholar](https://leapscholar.com/blog/?p=86136)) probably does not satisfy the one-sitting rule (verify).

**Test at a venue, not OET@Home** (`oet-04`) — OET@Home is accepted only for applicants with no physical test venue in their country [2027 FAQ](https://apps.wdoms.org/certification-pathways/faqs.html). OET has 19 test venues in India [OET](https://oet.com/post/oet-opens-new-test-venues-in-india-and-pakistan), so Indian applicants must test in person.

**Fallback: computer-based December slot** (`oet-05`) — Computer-at-venue OET runs in Monday–Saturday blocks, bookings close 7 days before, and results can arrive in about 48 hours [TalkDrill, secondary](https://www.talkdrill.com/blog/exams/oet/exam-dates-2026/). Which Indian venues offer computer-based OET in December 2026 is unconfirmed (verify).

**Fit OET practice into your Step 1 plan** (`oet-06`) — Add about 45 minutes a day of OET practice in weeks 2–5 of the Step 1 sprint and keep the day before and the day of OET light. Rescheduling costs AUD 120 if more than 7 days ahead, AUD 200 within 7 days [LeapScholar](https://leapscholar.com/blog/?p=86136).

**Submit the 2027 Pathway application by 31 Jan 2027 (ET)** (`oet-07`) — The application must be submitted no later than 31 Jan 2027, Eastern Time [ECFMG](https://apps.wdoms.org/certification-pathways/). The 2027 fee is reported as $945, non-refundable (up from $925) [The Match Guy, secondary](https://thematchguy.com/pathways-ecfmg-certification/). No Step 1 or Step 2 CK pass is needed to apply [2027 FAQ](https://apps.wdoms.org/certification-pathways/faqs.html).

**Confirm ECFMG has received your OET scores** (`oet-08`) — ECFMG must receive scores directly from OET by 31 Jan 2027; later results may not be processed in time [ECFMG](https://apps.wdoms.org/certification-pathways/). OET results typically arrive about 10 business days after the test [ECFMG FAQ](https://ecfmg.org/certification-pathways/faqs.html).

**Pathway 3 only: get the school attestation in** (`oet-09`) — A school official must attest to your clinical skills; electronic attestations are due 15 Feb 2027 and paper forms by 31 Jan 2027 [ECFMG](https://certification.ecfmg.org/certification-pathways/pathways-3-5.html). Schools submit through ECFMG's Clinical Skills Evaluation and Attestation Portal [ECFMG News](https://www.ecfmg.org/news/?p=5551).

**Pathway 6 fallback: arrange six in-person Mini-CEX encounters** (`oet-10`) — Pathway 6 is for applicants who do not meet Pathways 1–5 and needs six real, in-person encounters, each evaluated by a licensed physician; telemedicine and standardized patients are not accepted [2027 FAQ](https://apps.wdoms.org/certification-pathways/faqs.html). Mini-CEX forms are reported due 15 Feb 2027 [The Match Guy, secondary](https://thematchguy.com/pathways-ecfmg-certification/), and evaluations from a 2026 application cannot be reused [2027 FAQ](https://apps.wdoms.org/certification-pathways/faqs.html).

**Email ECFMG to confirm your Pathway's expiry** (`oet-11`) — A secondary source reports 2027 Pathways expire 31 Dec 2029 [The Match Guy](https://thematchguy.com/pathways-ecfmg-certification/); ECFMG's official table stops at the 2026 season, which expires 31 Dec 2028 [ECFMG](https://corp.ecfmg.org/certification-pathways/expiration.html). You must hold an accepted, unexpired Pathway at the Rank Order List deadline [ECFMG](https://corp.ecfmg.org/certification-pathways/expiration.html). Ask ECFMG in writing for the exact expiry and whether a 2027 Pathway stays valid if you skip the 2027 Match (verify).

**Watch for a 2028 Pathways announcement** (`oet-12`) — ECFMG publishes Pathways one year at a time and tells future applicants to “monitor the ECFMG website” [2027 FAQ](https://apps.wdoms.org/certification-pathways/faqs.html) [ECFMG News](https://www.ecfmg.org/news/category/pathways/). If you missed the 2027 window, a 2028 Pathway (not yet announced) is the fallback; the 2027 window accepted OET taken on or after 1 Jan 2025, so a Dec 2026 OET would probably still count (inferred, verify).

## Common mistakes

- Booking OET with your USMLE ID instead of your MyIntealth ID [2027 FAQ](https://apps.wdoms.org/certification-pathways/faqs.html).
- Planning on OET@Home — not accepted where a country has venues, and India has 19 [2027 FAQ](https://apps.wdoms.org/certification-pathways/faqs.html) [OET](https://oet.com/post/oet-opens-new-test-venues-in-india-and-pakistan).
- Thinking you need Step passes before applying for a Pathway — you don't [2027 FAQ](https://apps.wdoms.org/certification-pathways/faqs.html).
- Assuming a sub-test retake satisfies the one-sitting rule (probably not; verify) [ECFMG FAQ](https://ecfmg.org/certification-pathways/faqs.html).
- Waiting for 2028 Pathways that have not been announced [ECFMG News](https://www.ecfmg.org/news/category/pathways/).
