# 15. After residency: fellowship, boards, waiver jobs, H-1B, green card

*Stage key `post_residency` · last verified 2026-11-15 · re-verify quarterly and immediately on policy news.*

A J-1 physician must spend two years in India or obtain a waiver [ECFMG](https://ecfmg.org/evsp/applying-general.html); India will not issue a NORI [CGI SF](https://www.cgisf.gov.in/page/statement-of-need-certificates-for-medical-doctors/). Most J-1 doctors take a 3-year Conrad 30 or HHS waiver job in a shortage area and switch to H-1B [USCIS](https://uscis.gov/working-united-states/students-and-exchange-visitors/conrad-30-waiver-program); the federal HHS waiver ran a months-long backlog in 2025–26 [KFF Health News](https://kffhealthnews.org/health-industry/hhs-exchange-visitor-program-visa-waiver-j1-h1b-delays-foreign-doctors-deadline/). The India green-card queue is long: EB-2 India was at 1 Nov 2013 in the Nov 2026 bulletin (verify on travel.state.gov) [F1Jobs](https://www.f1jobs.io/resources/blog/november-2026-visa-bulletin-explained-eb2-eb3-india).

## Task details

**Decide on fellowship in PGY-2** (`post-01`) — In the 2027 ERAS Fellowships season, the July cycle opened to applicants on 1 Jul 2026 with program review from 15 Jul; the December cycle opened 12 Nov 2026 [AAMC](https://students-residents.aamc.org/eras-tools-and-worksheets-fellowship-applicants/2027-eras-fellowship-application-timeline). IM subspecialties use the July cycle, so a July-start resident applies early in PGY-3 and prepares in PGY-2 (inference).

**Know the Medicine Specialties Match calendar** (`post-02`) — In the fall 2026 Medicine & Pediatric Specialties Match, rank-list certification was due 18 Nov 2026, 9 p.m. ET, and Match Day was 2 Dec 2026 [NRMP](https://www.nrmp.org/about/news/2026/11/november-2026-illuminator-newsletter/). Expect a similar November–December pattern in your fellowship year.

**Check J-1 time left before choosing fellowship** (`post-03`) — ECFMG J-1 sponsorship is generally limited to seven years and the SoN must name the exact training specialty [ECFMG EVSP](https://ecfmg.org/evsp/applying-general.html). Programs longer than 7 years in total need an Exceptional Need Certificate from MoHFW [MoHFW](https://mohfw.gov.in/sites/default/files/Revised%20guidelines%20for%20issuance%20of%20SON%2C%20ENC%20and%20NORI_0.pdf).

**Confirm board eligibility** (`post-04`) — ABIM eligibility requires completed accredited training plus a full, unrestricted state medical license in good standing [ABIM](https://www.abim.org/certification/becoming-certified-in-internal-medicine/confirm-your-eligibility/). Plan the full license (see Step 3 and licensure) well before your final year ends.

**Choose: waiver job or two years in India** (`post-05`) — J-1 physicians must complete two years' physical presence in the home country before certain status changes unless waived [ECFMG](https://ecfmg.org/evsp/applying-general.html). India will not issue a NORI [CGI SF](https://www.cgisf.gov.in/page/statement-of-need-certificates-for-medical-doctors/), so a Conrad 30, HHS or similar waiver is the route to stay; without one you must leave and wait two years before an H-1B [KFF Health News](https://kffhealthnews.org/health-industry/hhs-exchange-visitor-program-visa-waiver-j1-h1b-delays-foreign-doctors-deadline/).

**Start the waiver job search in PGY-2** (`post-06`) — Conrad 30 needs a full-time (40 h/week) H-1B contract for at least 3 years at a HPSA, MUA or MUP facility, starting within 90 days of the waiver [USCIS](https://uscis.gov/working-united-states/students-and-exchange-visitors/conrad-30-waiver-program). 3RNet publishes a J-1 waiver job-search timeline, but the page was login-gated and its contents are unverified [3RNet](https://www.3rnet.org/resources/j1-waiver).

**California Conrad 30: apply 1–31 October** (`post-07`) — HCAI's 2026–27 window was 1–31 Oct 2026, with a secondary 1–31 Dec cycle if slots remain; applications are reviewed in order of receipt with rural priority and no FLEX slots [HCAI](https://hcai.ca.gov/workforce-capacity/california-primary-care-office/j-1-visa-waiver-program/). Practice must be full-time in a HPSA, MUA or MUP (MHPSA for psychiatry) [HCAI](https://hcai.ca.gov/workforce-capacity/california-primary-care-office/j-1-visa-waiver-program/).

**Apply in more than one state and do not rely on HHS speed** (`post-08`) — The HHS clinical waiver stopped in late 2025 and resumed “dramatically slower”, leaving hundreds backlogged vs 1–3 weeks historically [KFF Health News](https://kffhealthnews.org/health-industry/hhs-exchange-visitor-program-visa-waiver-j1-h1b-delays-foreign-doctors-deadline/). A June 2026 Senate letter described an “administrative bottleneck” with no HHS timeline [Sen. Gillibrand](https://www.gillibrand.senate.gov/wp-content/uploads/2026/06/26.06.02-Letter-to-HHS-re-J-1-Visa-Processing-Delays-and-Physician-Workforce-Shortages.pdf). California also issues HHS support letters year-round for primary care (FM, general IM, peds, OB/GYN, psychiatry) [HCAI](https://hcai.ca.gov/workforce-capacity/california-primary-care-office/j-1-visa-waiver-program/).

**Change status from J-1 to H-1B for the waiver job** (`post-09`) — The H-1B cap does not apply to physicians changing status under a state- or federal-agency-requested waiver [8 USC 1184](https://www.law.cornell.edu/uscode/text/8/1184). The $100,000 proclamation does not apply to a J-1 to H-1B change of status [FSMB/attorneys](https://www.fsmb.org/siteassets/baw-speaker-presentation/session-8-imgs-fsmb-attorneys-nov-2025.pdf). Avoid travel while the change is pending [Intealth](https://certification.ecfmg.org/evsp/applying-pre-arrival.html).

**Watch the 30-day grace period** (`post-10`) — After training ends you have 30 days to depart, with no work or training authorization during that time [ECFMG EVSP](https://ecfmg.org/evsp/applying-general.html). In 2026, doctors waiting on HHS waivers likely had to reach USCIS by 30 July or leave [KFF Health News](https://kffhealthnews.org/health-industry/hhs-exchange-visitor-program-visa-waiver-j1-h1b-delays-foreign-doctors-deadline/).

**Start the green-card plan early** (`post-11`) — Nov 2026 Visa Bulletin (India): EB-1 1 Feb 2023, EB-2 1 Nov 2013, EB-3 22 Jan 2014; 356,360 approved EB-2 India petitions were waiting as of June 2026 (unofficial; verify on travel.state.gov) [F1Jobs](https://www.f1jobs.io/resources/blog/november-2026-visa-bulletin-explained-eb2-eb3-india). EB-2 India Final Action was “unavailable” in the July 2026 bulletin [National Law Review](https://natlawreview.com/article/july-2026-visa-bulletin-shows-unavailability-india-eb-2-final-action-dates). Filing an I-140 early to lock a priority date is advised (inference); EB-1 or a US-citizen/green-card spouse are the faster routes.

**Know cap-subject vs cap-exempt employers** (`post-12`) — Cap exemptions cover institutions of higher education, related nonprofits and nonprofit or government research organizations [8 USC 1184](https://www.law.cornell.edu/uscode/text/8/1184) [Ogletree](https://ogletree.com/insights-resources/blog-posts/potential-h-1b-cap-exempt-options-for-healthcare-employers/). Community hospitals not affiliated with a university are usually cap-subject (inference).

**H-4 spouse work rules if you move to H-1B** (`post-13`) — An H-4 spouse can get an EAD only if the H-1B principal has an approved I-140 or AC21 extensions; renewal can be filed up to 180 days ahead [USCIS](https://uscis.gov/node/48037).

**Redesignate Indian bank accounts** (`post-14`) — On becoming non-resident, existing resident accounts should be redesignated NRO; NRE accounts are repatriable and their interest is exempt from income tax [RBI](https://www.rbi.org.in/Scripts/FAQDisplay.aspx?Id=52). NRIs cannot remit under LRS [ICICI](https://www.icici.bank.in/nri-banking/nriedge/nri-articles/lrs-for-sending-money-abroad-from-india).

**Brief your sureties if you will not return** (`post-15`) — The MoHFW bond and undertaking carry a ₹10 lakh demand plus 12% interest on default [MoHFW](https://mohfw.gov.in/sites/default/files/Revised%20guidelines%20for%20issuance%20of%20SON%2C%20ENC%20and%20NORI_0.pdf). How MoHFW enforces it against waiver recipients is unknown, so tell your sureties plainly that their exposure is real.

**Get a personal DEA registration for attending practice** (`post-16`) — During residency you can prescribe under the hospital's DEA number with a suffix [21 CFR 1301.22](https://www.law.cornell.edu/cfr/text/21/1301.22); a personal registration matters mainly afterwards (inference). Apply once your full state license is issued.

## Common mistakes

- Starting the waiver job search in the final months of residency.
- Relying on HHS processing speed [KFF Health News](https://kffhealthnews.org/health-industry/hhs-exchange-visitor-program-visa-waiver-j1-h1b-delays-foreign-doctors-deadline/).
- Travelling while a J-1→H-1B change of status is pending [Intealth](https://certification.ecfmg.org/evsp/applying-pre-arrival.html).
- Assuming a physician NIW makes the India green-card wait short [F1Jobs](https://www.f1jobs.io/resources/blog/november-2026-visa-bulletin-explained-eb2-eb3-india).
