# 13. Residency life

*Stage key `residency_life` · last verified 2026-11-15 · re-verify quarterly and immediately on policy news.*

ACGME caps work at 80 hours a week averaged over 4 weeks, with one day in seven free [ACGME](https://acgme.org/newsroom/2017/3/acgme-releases-revised-common-program-requirements-section-vi-the-learning-and-working-environment-). PGY-1 pay averaged $68,166 nationally and $77,649 in the West (AAMC, Jul 2025) [AMA](https://www.ama-assn.org/medical-residents/residency-life/resident-physician-pay-still-rising-growth-trails-inflation); Bay Area programs pay $84k–$102k [UCSF](https://meded.ucsf.edu/gme-resident-and-fellow-resource-hub/salary-and-benefits) [FREIDA](https://freida.ama-assn.org/program/1400531051/features-benefits). J-1 residents cannot moonlight [ECFMG](https://ecfmg.org/evsp/applying-general.html), and any trip abroad needs a travel-signed DS-2019 and a valid J-1 stamp [Intealth](https://ecfmg.org/evsp/during-travel.html).

## Task details

**Know your duty-hour limits** (`res-01`) — ACGME limits: at most 80 hours a week averaged over 4 weeks, 24 continuous hours plus up to 4 for transitions, one day in seven free, and in-house call no more often than every third night [ACGME](https://acgme.org/newsroom/2017/3/acgme-releases-revised-common-program-requirements-section-vi-the-learning-and-working-environment-). Specialties may set stricter limits.

**Do not moonlight on a J-1** (`res-02`) — Unauthorized employment, including moonlighting, is prohibited for ECFMG J-1 physicians [ECFMG](https://ecfmg.org/evsp/applying-general.html). Texas PIT permits also bar outside moonlighting [TMB](https://www.tmb.texas.gov/apply-renew/physician/physician-apply/physician-in-training-permit-application).

**Benchmark your pay** (`res-03`) — AAMC's 2025 survey put the national PGY-1 mean at $68,166; regional averages were Northeast $74,994, Southern $65,076, Central $68,580 and Western $77,649 [AMA](https://www.ama-assn.org/medical-residents/residency-life/resident-physician-pay-still-rising-growth-trails-inflation). Use this to sanity-check your budget and offers.

**Get a travel signature before any trip abroad** (`res-04`) — Re-entry requires a DS-2019 with a travel signature, valid one year or until the DS-2019 end date, whichever comes first; only Intealth Regional Advisors can sign [Intealth](https://ecfmg.org/evsp/during-travel.html). If you lack one, request a duplicate DS-2019, processed within 5 business days in MyIntealth, before booking a visa appointment [Intealth](https://ecfmg.org/evsp/during-travel.html).

**Plan any visa renewal in India months ahead** (`res-05`) — A renewal abroad needs the DS-160, fees, photo, passport, a paper travel-validated DS-2019 and evidence of nonimmigrant intent; State Department security checks take about 5 business days to 3+ months [Intealth](https://ecfmg.org/evsp/during-travel.html). Re-entry needs a valid J-1 stamp [Intealth](https://certification.ecfmg.org/evsp/applying-pre-arrival.html); avoid casual trips home if your stamp has expired (inference).

**Know automatic revalidation for short trips** (`res-06`) — Automatic revalidation covers trips of under 30 days to Canada, Mexico or adjacent islands, but not if a new visa application is pending or was denied there [Intealth](https://ecfmg.org/evsp/during-travel.html). Avoid any travel while a change of status is pending [Intealth](https://certification.ecfmg.org/evsp/applying-pre-arrival.html).

**File your US tax return by 15 April** (`res-07`) — File a federal return by 15 April even with no US income [Intealth](https://certification.ecfmg.org/evsp/applying-pre-arrival.html). Attach Form 8843 to Form 1040-NR for each exempt year, or mail it alone to the IRS in Austin, TX 73301-0215 [IRS Pub 519](https://www.irs.gov/publications/p519).

**Expect FICA to start after two calendar years** (`res-08`) — After the first 2 calendar years, J-1 physicians generally become resident aliens for tax purposes and lose the FICA exemption [IRS](https://irs.gov/individuals/international-taxpayers/aliens-employed-in-the-u-s-social-security-taxes). Adjust your budget for the 7.65% deduction from that point (inference on timing).

**Start retirement saving** (`res-09`) — The 2026 403(b) elective deferral limit is $24,500, and 457 plans are a separate bucket outside the combined 403(b)/401(k) limit [IRS](https://www.irs.gov/node/5671). The 457(b) limit itself was not confirmed in the sources (verify).

**Renew the J-2 EAD on time** (`res-10`) — A J-2 EAD renewal can be filed only after the J-1's DS-2019 is extended [MIT ISchO](https://ischo.mit.edu/information-daily-life/information-spouses-partners-and-families/j-2-work-permission). Since 12 Dec 2025 USCIS no longer accepts self-submitted photos, so biometrics may be requested [MIT ISchO](https://ischo.mit.edu/information-daily-life/information-spouses-partners-and-families/j-2-work-permission).

**Save the Physician Support Line** (`res-11`) — The Physician Support Line, 1-888-409-0141, is free, confidential and staffed by volunteer psychiatrists for US physicians; it is not a crisis line, so in a crisis call or text 988 or 911 [Physician Support Line](https://www.physiciansupportline.com/).

## Common mistakes

- Moonlighting on a J-1 [ECFMG](https://ecfmg.org/evsp/applying-general.html).
- Booking a trip home without a valid travel signature or visa stamp [Intealth](https://ecfmg.org/evsp/during-travel.html).
- Forgetting Form 8843 and the 15 April filing [IRS](https://www.irs.gov/publications/p519).
- Forgetting MyIntealth address updates within 10 days [Intealth](https://certification.ecfmg.org/evsp/applying-pre-arrival.html).
