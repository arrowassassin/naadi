# 10. J-1 visa (and the H-1B branch)

*Stage key `visa_j1` · last verified 2026-11-15 · re-verify quarterly and immediately on policy news.*

J-1 is the default residency visa. It needs ECFMG/Intealth sponsorship and a Statement of Need from MoHFW naming the exact specialty, is generally capped at 7 years, and carries the INA 212(e) two-year home-residency rule [ECFMG EVSP](https://ecfmg.org/evsp/applying-general.html). MoHFW will not issue a NORI “under any circumstance” [CGI San Francisco](https://www.cgisf.gov.in/page/statement-of-need-certificates-for-medical-doctors/), and each SoN needs a ₹10 lakh surety bond with two sureties [MoHFW](https://mohfw.gov.in/sites/default/files/Revised%20guidelines%20for%20issuance%20of%20SON%2C%20ENC%20and%20NORI_0.pdf). In 2026 some Indian matched residents faced refusals and long administrative processing [South Asian Herald](https://southasianherald.com/raja-krishnamoorthi-raises-concerns-over-j-1-visa-delays-for-indian-doctors/), so start every step the week after Match. H-1B for residency needs Step 3 before the petition and is exposed to the $100,000 fee litigation [FSMB/attorneys](https://www.fsmb.org/siteassets/baw-speaker-presentation/session-8-imgs-fsmb-attorneys-nov-2025.pdf).

## Task details

**Apply to MoHFW for the Statement of Need** (`visa-01`) — The SoN is processed only by MoHFW in New Delhi, not by consulates: Under Secretary (IC), International Cooperation Section, Room 514, 'A' Wing, Nirman Bhavan, New Delhi 110011; soic-mohfw@nic.in; +91-11-2306-3068 / 2306-1945 [CGI San Francisco](https://www.cgisf.gov.in/page/statement-of-need-certificates-for-medical-doctors/). MoHFW's processing time, application fee and document checklist were not found in any source (verify), so apply the week after Match.

**Make sure the SoN wording is exactly right** (`visa-02`) — The SoN must be on official letterhead, addressed to ECFMG, use the exact 22 CFR §62.27 wording, name a specialty that exactly matches your training specialty, and carry an official stamp and dated signature [ECFMG EVSP](https://ecfmg.org/evsp/applying-general.html). It must arrive in a sealed Ministry envelope or as a PDF from an official Ministry email [ECFMG EVSP](https://ecfmg.org/evsp/applying-general.html). A later specialty switch needs a new SoN.

**Arrange the ₹10 lakh surety bond and two sureties** (`visa-03`) — Each SoN needs a separate surety bond of ₹10 lakh on ₹100 non-judicial stamp paper, with two financially sound sureties: one close relative who is an Indian citizen in India and one unrelated person [MoHFW OM 2023](https://mohfw.gov.in/sites/default/files/Revised%20guidelines%20for%20issuance%20of%20SON%2C%20ENC%20and%20NORI_0.pdf). The ₹10 lakh is a contingent liability, not a fee; only the stamp paper (about $1) is a cost.

**Understand the bond's return-to-India clause** (`visa-04`) — The MoHFW guidelines include a clause to report back to India within 3 months of completing training (extendable by 3 months), with a penalty demand of ₹10 lakh plus 12% annual interest; the scan is partly illegible (verify against the original) [MoHFW](https://mohfw.gov.in/sites/default/files/Revised%20guidelines%20for%20issuance%20of%20SON%2C%20ENC%20and%20NORI_0.pdf). The SoN is capped at a combined 7 years on J-1; longer programs need an Exceptional Need Certificate [MoHFW](https://mohfw.gov.in/sites/default/files/Revised%20guidelines%20for%20issuance%20of%20SON%2C%20ENC%20and%20NORI_0.pdf). Brief your sureties on their exposure.

**Know the J-1 rules before you sign anything** (`visa-05`) — ECFMG sponsorship is generally limited to 7 years, authorizes one training activity, location and compensation, allows one specialty change in the first two years, and prohibits moonlighting [ECFMG EVSP](https://ecfmg.org/evsp/applying-general.html). J-1 physicians and J-2 dependents are subject to INA 212(e) [ECFMG EVSP](https://ecfmg.org/evsp/applying-general.html), and MoHFW will not issue a NORI “under any circumstance” [CGI SF](https://www.cgisf.gov.in/page/statement-of-need-certificates-for-medical-doctors/).

**Complete the Intealth J-1 application with your program** (`visa-06`) — After approval the DS-2019 appears in MyIntealth for you and your TPL [Intealth](https://certification.ecfmg.org/evsp/applying-pre-arrival.html). The current Intealth sponsorship fee is unverified; programs often pay it, so confirm with your GME office (verify).

**Print and sign the DS-2019** (`visa-07`) — Print the DS-2019 single-page, double-sided, and sign it in ink [Intealth](https://certification.ecfmg.org/evsp/applying-pre-arrival.html). Keep the paper original for the interview and for every future entry.

**Pay the SEVIS I-901 fee** (`visa-08`) — The I-901 fee for J exchange visitors is $220 (full payment), paid at ice.gov/sevis/i901 [ICE](https://ice.gov/sevis/i901); J-2 dependents are not charged separately [Intealth](https://certification.ecfmg.org/evsp/applying-pre-arrival.html). The $220 rate took effect 24 Jun 2019 [BAL](https://www.bal.com/immigration-news/ice-to-increase-fees-on-f-and-m-students-j-exchange-visitors-among-others/). Save the receipt.

**File the DS-160 and pay the $185 visa fee** (`visa-09`) — Complete the DS-160 and pay the $185 visa fee [Intealth](https://certification.ecfmg.org/evsp/applying-pre-arrival.html). Since June 2025, F/M/J applicants must set social-media accounts to public and list all usernames from the past 5 years on the DS-160 [Business Today, secondary](https://www.businesstoday.in/nri/visa/story/planning-a-us-trip-five-major-visa-updates-you-must-know-before-applying-499476-2025-10-24).

**Book the earliest J-1 interview at any Indian post** (`visa-10`) — You must interview in India [Faegre Drinker](https://www.faegredrinker.com/en/insights/publications/2025/9/new-home-country-only-visa-interview-rule-and-termination-of-the-interview-waiver). In June 2026 Intealth encouraged Indian physicians previously denied an appointment to reapply for a J visa at a post in India, while stressing decisions are at the consular officer's discretion [AAIM](https://www.im.org/about/alliance-news/article/2026/06/11/intealth-shares-update-on-visa-appointments-for-physicians-from-india). Whether physicians are formally prioritized in 2026 is unconfirmed (verify).

**Assemble the interview document set** (`visa-11`) — Bring a passport valid 6 months beyond the DS-2019 end date, the signed DS-2019, financial evidence (contract or offer letter, funding letter, bank statement), the SEVIS receipt, a 2×2 inch photo and the DS-160 confirmation [Intealth](https://certification.ecfmg.org/evsp/applying-pre-arrival.html). You must show a permanent residence abroad, strong ties and how the training will be useful at home [Intealth](https://certification.ecfmg.org/evsp/applying-pre-arrival.html).

**Prepare to explain earlier B-visa observerships** (`visa-12`) — Reports in 2026 described matched Indian residents facing visa refusals, prolonged administrative processing or cancelled visitor visas [South Asian Herald](https://southasianherald.com/raja-krishnamoorthi-raises-concerns-over-j-1-visa-delays-for-indian-doctors/). Intealth has explained to agencies that observerships and J-1 training are distinct [AAIM](https://www.im.org/about/alliance-news/article/2026/06/11/intealth-shares-update-on-visa-appointments-for-physicians-from-india). Be ready to describe past visits as clinical observation only (inference; the cause of cancellations was not confirmed).

**Plan for administrative processing and a late start** (`visa-13`) — Your visa may go into administrative processing [Intealth](https://certification.ecfmg.org/evsp/applying-pre-arrival.html). In July 2026 more than 20 medical societies said visa delays were keeping foreign-born residents from 1 July starts [The Cancer Letter](https://cancerletter.com/cancer-policy/20260702_5c/). If your interview falls after mid-June, tell your program coordinator immediately.

**Collect your passport** (`visa-14`) — Since August 2025, passports in New Delhi must be collected in person (secondary) [Business Today](https://www.businesstoday.in/nri/visa/story/planning-a-us-trip-five-major-visa-updates-you-must-know-before-applying-499476-2025-10-24). Check the J-1 annotation and your name against the DS-2019 before leaving the counter.

**Register J-2 dependents correctly** (`visa-15`) — J-2 dependents are also subject to 212(e) and to the insurance minimums [ECFMG](https://ecfmg.org/evsp/applying-general.html). J-2s are not charged a separate SEVIS fee [Intealth](https://certification.ecfmg.org/evsp/applying-pre-arrival.html).

**H-1B branch: confirm Step 3 and the petition timeline** (`visa-16`) — An H-1B for residency generally needs Step 3 passed before the petition is filed [FSMB/attorneys](https://www.fsmb.org/siteassets/baw-speaker-presentation/session-8-imgs-fsmb-attorneys-nov-2025.pdf). Teaching hospitals tied to universities or nonprofits are usually cap-exempt [Ogletree](https://ogletree.com/insights-resources/blog-posts/potential-h-1b-cap-exempt-options-for-healthcare-employers/), so the wage-weighted lottery (in effect 27 Feb 2026) rarely applies [Ogletree](https://ogletree.com/insights-resources/blog-posts/uscis-completes-fiscal-year-2027-h-1b-lottery/).

**H-1B branch: check the $100,000 fee status before relying on it** (`visa-17`) — Proclamation 10973 applies to new petitions for physicians outside the US, which describes you if you match from India; the employer pays [FSMB/attorneys](https://www.fsmb.org/siteassets/baw-speaker-presentation/session-8-imgs-fsmb-attorneys-nov-2025.pdf). Vacated 8 Jun 2026 with a stay denied 24 Jul [Clark Hill](https://www.clarkhill.com/news-events/news/first-circuit-blocks-reinstatement-of-100k-h-1b-fee/), renewed 18 Sep 2026 to 21 Sep 2027 [Klasko](https://www.klaskolaw.com/h-1b-100000-fee-extended-one-year/), then vacated and enjoined 30 Sep 2026 [Forbes](https://www.forbes.com/sites/stuartanderson/2026/10/01/judge-strikes-down-latest-version-of-100000-h-1b-immigration-fee/). As of Nov 2026 it is not being collected, but litigation and a proposed $103,265 fee rule continue.

**H-1B branch: expect longer consular vetting** (`visa-18`) — Online-presence review was extended to all H-1B/H-4 applicants from 15 Dec 2025, and thousands of Indian H-1B interviews were rescheduled to Mar–May 2026 [The PIE News](https://thepienews.com/india-new-vetting-delays-us-h-1b-interviews-to-summer-2026/) [Global Immigration Blog](https://www.globalimmigrationblog.com/2025/12/h%e2%80%911b-h%e2%80%914-visa-appointments-rescheduling-begins-amid-expanded-social-media-vetting-policies/). Book the H-1B stamping appointment the day the I-797 approval arrives.

## Common mistakes

- A SoN that does not name the exact training specialty [ECFMG](https://ecfmg.org/evsp/applying-general.html).
- Leaving surety arrangements until after the Match.
- Expecting India to issue a NORI [CGI SF](https://www.cgisf.gov.in/page/statement-of-need-certificates-for-medical-doctors/).
- Assuming the $100k H-1B fee is gone for good [Forbes](https://www.forbes.com/sites/stuartanderson/2026/10/01/judge-strikes-down-latest-version-of-100000-h-1b-immigration-fee/).
- Inconsistent DS-160 and social-media history [Business Today](https://www.businesstoday.in/nri/visa/story/planning-a-us-trip-five-major-visa-updates-you-must-know-before-applying-499476-2025-10-24).
