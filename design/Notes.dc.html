Acid–base in five moves

Always the same order: pH, primary process, gap, compensation, delta. Anion gap = Na⁺ − (Cl⁻ + HCO₃⁻); normal is roughly 8–12.

Winters: expected PCO₂ = 1.5 × HCO₃⁻ + 8 ± 2. Higher than expected means an added respiratory acidosis; lower means an added respiratory alkalosis.

I keep skipping the delta step. Compare the rise in gap with the fall in bicarbonate before answering.
