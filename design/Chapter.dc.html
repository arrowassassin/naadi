Same acidosis,
three different leaks.

Every renal tubular acidosis is a normal anion gap metabolic acidosis. What separates them is where the tubule fails — and what happens to potassium.

TypeDefectUrine pHSerum K⁺
1 · Distalα-intercalated cells can't secrete H⁺> 5.5Low
2 · ProximalProximal tubule can't reabsorb HCO₃⁻< 5.5Low
4 · HyperkalemicLow aldosterone, or resistance to it< 5.5High

Type 1 is the one that forms calcium phosphate stones. Type 2 often arrives as part of Fanconi syndrome.
