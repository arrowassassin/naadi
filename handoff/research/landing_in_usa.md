# Landing in the USA: Match Day to post-residency for an Indian J-1/H-1B resident (as of 15 Nov 2026)

Scope note: this extends "/home/claude/reports/India MBBS to US residency roadmap.md" and does not repeat its exam, H-1B $100k-fee litigation, Conrad 30 (California, applications 1–31 Oct), green-card backlog (EB-2 India at 1 Nov 2013 in the Nov 2026 bulletin) or California PTL/24-month content. The research budget (about 23 tool calls) did not reach every sub-question. Items not verified are listed under Gaps and must not be published as fact.

## 1. Match Day to departure (March–June)

### Takeaway
The critical path is: contract → ECFMG/Intealth J-1 application (which needs the MoHFW Statement of Need, backed by a ₹10 lakh surety bond with two sureties) → DS-2019 in MyIntealth → SEVIS I-901 → DS-160 → J-1 interview in India. Indian matched residents faced refusals, long administrative processing and revoked visitor visas in 2026. The single biggest risk is a late visa, so every step should start the week after Match.

### Cited Findings
**ECFMG/Intealth J-1 sponsorship**
- ECFMG sponsorship requires a Statement of Need (SoN) from the central Ministry of Health of the country of most recent legal permanent residence. The SoN must be on official letterhead, addressed to ECFMG, and use the exact wording in 22 CFR §62.27. It must name a specialty that *exactly* matches the training specialty, and it needs an official stamp or seal and a dated signature. The ECFMG page was last updated 17 Mar 2021. — [ECFMG EVSP general](https://ecfmg.org/evsp/applying-general.html)
- Sponsorship is "generally limited to seven years." It authorizes one specific training activity, location and compensation. Unauthorized employment ("moonlighting") is prohibited. There is no work or training authorization during the 30-day grace period. — [ECFMG EVSP general](https://ecfmg.org/evsp/applying-general.html)
- Mandatory health insurance for the J-1 and for J-2 dependents:
  - At least $100,000 per accident or illness
  - Deductible of no more than $500
  - Co-insurance of no more than 25%
  - ECFMG provides repatriation ($25,000) and medical evacuation ($50,000) coverage.
  - Source: [ECFMG EVSP general](https://ecfmg.org/evsp/applying-general.html)
- Insurance must start on the DS-2019 program start date and continue without a break. — [Intealth pre-arrival page, updated 27 Feb 2026](https://certification.ecfmg.org/evsp/applying-pre-arrival.html)
- Sequence after approval (Intealth pre-arrival page, updated 27 Feb 2026, [link](https://certification.ecfmg.org/evsp/applying-pre-arrival.html)):
  1. The DS-2019 appears in MyIntealth for the physician and the Training Program Liaison (TPL).
  2. Print it single-page, double-sided, and sign it in ink.
  3. Pay the SEVIS fee at ice.gov/sevis/i901 (J-2s are not charged separately).
  4. Complete the DS-160, pay the visa fee of $185, and attend the interview.
  5. The visa may go into administrative processing.
  6. Enter the US no earlier than 30 days before the DS-2019 start date.
- Visa interview documents:
  - Passport valid at least 6 months beyond the DS-2019 end date
  - Signed DS-2019
  - Financial evidence: contract or offer letter, funding letter, bank statement
  - SEVIS receipt
  - 2×2 in photo
  - DS-160 confirmation
  - The applicant must show a permanent residence abroad, strong ties, and how the training will be useful at home (nonimmigrant intent).
  - Source: [Intealth pre-arrival](https://certification.ecfmg.org/evsp/applying-pre-arrival.html)

**MoHFW Statement of Need (India)**
- SoN and ENC applications are processed only by MoHFW, not by consulates. Contact: Under Secretary (IC), International Cooperation Section, MoHFW, Room 514, 'A' Wing, Nirman Bhavan, New Delhi 110011; soic-mohfw@nic.in; +91-11-2306-3068 / 2306-1945. — [CGI San Francisco](https://www.cgisf.gov.in/page/statement-of-need-certificates-for-medical-doctors/)
- A NORI "would not be issued by MOH&FW under any circumstance." — [CGI San Francisco](https://www.cgisf.gov.in/page/statement-of-need-certificates-for-medical-doctors/)
- MoHFW OM L-20019/1(Policy)/2013-LC (July 2023; supersedes the 03.08.2021 OM) [link](https://mohfw.gov.in/sites/default/files/Revised%20guidelines%20for%20issuance%20of%20SON%2C%20ENC%20and%20NORI_0.pdf):
  - SoN is capped at a combined 7 years on J-1 (first issue plus extensions).
  - Each SoN needs a separate **surety bond of ₹10 lakh** on ₹100 non-judicial stamp paper.
  - The bond needs **two sureties**: one close relative who is an Indian citizen in India, and one unrelated person, both financially sound.
  - Programs longer than 7 years in total need an Exceptional Need Certificate (ENC).
  - The guidelines also contain a reporting-back-to-India clause: report within 3 months of completion, extendable by 3 months. Penalties are demand of ₹10 lakh plus 12% annual interest. The scan is partly illegible, so verify against the original before publishing.

**2026 visa environment for Indian physicians**
- 22 May 2026: Rep. Raja Krishnamoorthi raised concerns about "unexpected" J-1 denials. OGKTMA reported a growing number of matched residents facing "visa refusals, prolonged administrative processing, or cancellations of previously valid visitor visas" ahead of July starts. No count of affected doctors was given. — [South Asian Herald](https://southasianherald.com/raja-krishnamoorthi-raises-concerns-over-j-1-visa-delays-for-indian-doctors/)

It encouraged physicians from India who had earlier been denied an appointment to reapply for a J visa at a post in India. It also stressed that decisions are solely at the consular officer's discretion. — [ACP/AAIM news](https://www.im.org/about/alliance-news/article/2026/06/11/intealth-shares-update-on-visa-appointments-for-physicians-from-india)
- Background: from December 2025, H-1B/H-4 appointments in India were rescheduled as social-media vetting expanded. — [Global Immigration Blog, Dec 2025](https://www.globalimmigrationblog.com/2025/12/h%e2%80%911b-h%e2%80%914-visa-appointments-rescheduling-begins-amid-expanded-social-media-vetting-policies/)

**Money out of India (RBI LRS)**
- Residents can remit up to **USD 250,000 per financial year**. Forex-card loads count toward the limit.
- TCS: the first **₹10 lakh per year is exempt**. Above that, TCS is 2% for education or medical purposes and **20% for "any other purpose."** TCS is creditable against income tax. The rates apply from 1 Apr 2026 under the Finance Act 2026.
- Permitted purposes include transfers to your *own* account abroad, maintenance and travel.
- Source: [ICICI Bank, updated 15 May 2026](https://www.icici.bank.in/nri-banking/nriedge/nri-articles/lrs-for-sending-money-abroad-from-india)

### Inferences
- Order of tasks: request the SoN from MoHFW immediately after Match, because the bond requires two sureties and stamp paper. Then complete the Intealth application (TPL-initiated), receive the DS-2019, pay I-901, fill the DS-160 and book the interview. Allow 6–10 weeks, plus a cushion for administrative processing.
- Many programs are delaying start dates to allow for visa issues. The site should advise residents to tell their program coordinator early if the interview falls after mid-June.
- Because of the 2026 refusals, applicants with past B-visa observerships should be ready to explain them as clinical-observation-only. Public social media should be consistent with the DS-160. This inference rests on reports of B-visa cancellations; the exact cause was not confirmed.
- Funding: a parent can remit about $11,000 (≈₹10 lakh) TCS-free per financial year. Larger amounts sent as "own account" or "maintenance" attract 20% TCS above ₹10 lakh. The TCS is refundable through the ITR but ties up cash, so splitting across family members' PANs or across two financial years (March/April) helps.

## 2. Arrival (first 30 days)

### Takeaway
At the airport, present passport, visa and DS-2019, and check that the I-94 reads "J-1 D/S." Report to the program immediately so the TPL can validate arrival in SEVIS. Wait 11 business days after validation (SSA says 10 days after arrival) before applying for an SSN. The SSN application is free and needs an in-person visit within 45 days of starting it online. A DHS rule replacing D/S with fixed admit-until dates (published 17 Jul 2026) is postponed as of 23 Sep 2026; watch it.

### Cited Findings
**Entry and I-94**
- At CBP, present passport, visa and DS-2019. The I-94 should read "J-1 D/S." If it shows a fixed date or a different status, contact Intealth immediately. Print the electronic I-94. — [Intealth pre-arrival](https://certification.ecfmg.org/evsp/applying-pre-arrival.html)

**Arrival validation**
- The TPL submits the arrival form plus copies of the visa and I-94 in MyIntealth, and Intealth validates the physician in SEVIS. No day count is given; the instruction is "as soon as possible." After validation, the program start date cannot be amended. — [Intealth pre-arrival](https://certification.ecfmg.org/evsp/applying-pre-arrival.html)
- Report address changes within 10 days through MyIntealth. — [Intealth pre-arrival](https://certification.ecfmg.org/evsp/applying-pre-arrival.html)

**Duration-of-status rule (watch item)**
- The DHS final rule was published 17 Jul 2026. It replaces D/S with a fixed Admit Until Date, generally the program end plus 30 days, with a maximum of 4 years per admission. Extensions would require an Extension of Stay on Form I-539 (filed up to 180 days ahead) or travel and re-entry.
- The rule was due to take effect 15 Sep 2026. As of a 23 Sep 2026 update it is postponed, with no new date.
- Physicians already in D/S generally see no immediate change.
- Source: [UT Health San Antonio OIS](https://wp.uthscsa.edu/ois/immigration/ecfmg-j-1/elimination-of-duration-of-status-impact-on-ecfmg-j-1-alien-physicians/); see also [Klasko presentation, 9 Sep 2026](https://www.klaskolaw.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/2026.09.09-Presentation-Duration-of-Status-Eliminated_Navigating-the-New-Fixed-Admission-Period_Healthcare-RG-TD-IW.pdf)

**Social Security Number**
- Intealth: apply after waiting **11 business days following SEVIS validation**, and select "other" for citizenship. — [Intealth pre-arrival](https://certification.ecfmg.org/evsp/applying-pre-arrival.html)
- SSA (Pub 05-10096, February 2026, [link](https://www.ssa.gov/pubs/EN-05-10096.pdf)):
  - Wait 10 days after arrival.
  - Start online at ssa.gov/number-card/request-number-firsttime, then visit an office within 45 days.
  - Bring originals: DS-2019, I-94 and passport. Photocopies and notarized copies are not accepted.
  - The service is **free**.
- USCIS paused "Enumeration Beyond Entry" in April 2025, so the SSN must be requested directly from SSA. — [MIT ISchO](https://ischo.mit.edu/information-daily-life/information-spouses-partners-and-families/j-2-work-permission)

**California driver license**
- Applicants need an original birth-date/legal-presence document valid at least 30 days to start and 60 days to receive a photo card. If legal presence ends within 5 years, a limited-term license is issued. An SSN is required only if the applicant is authorized to work. The card is mailed after the tests are passed and USCIS verifies status. — [CA DMV legal presence](https://www.dmv.ca.gov/portal/driver-licenses-identification-cards/limited-term-for-legal-presence/)

**Taxes (cited)**
- Under the J-1 FICA exemption, nonresident aliens are exempt from Social Security and Medicare tax on wages for services "performed to carry out the purposes for which they were admitted." The exemption ends when the person becomes a resident alien for tax purposes. It does **not** apply to J-2s. The IRS page names professors, teachers and researchers but not physicians. — [IRS](https://irs.gov/individuals/international-taxpayers/aliens-employed-in-the-u-s-social-security-taxes)
- Article 21(2) of the US–India treaty lets an Indian nonresident who is in the US mainly for education or training claim the standard deduction. Article 22 exempts teachers and researchers for 2 years, retroactively lost if the stay exceeds 2 years. The source does not say whether residents qualify. — [Sprintax](https://blog.sprintax.com/us-india-tax-treaty/)
- File a federal return by 15 April even with no US income. — [Intealth pre-arrival](https://certification.ecfmg.org/evsp/applying-pre-arrival.html)

### Inferences
- J-1 physicians are "non-students" (teacher/trainee category). Under IRS Pub 519 they are generally exempt individuals for the substantial-presence test for 2 of the preceding 6 calendar years. They would therefore be nonresident aliens, filing Form 1040-NR plus Form 8843 and generally exempt from FICA, for roughly the first 2 calendar years. Many GME tax guides say this, for example [UConn GME international tax guidelines](https://health.uconn.edu/graduate-medical-education/wp-content/uploads/sites/20/2020/06/International-Tax-Guidelines.pdf) (not fetched). This was not confirmed from Pub 519 itself in this research, so it must be verified before publishing.
- FICA is 7.65%, so 2 years of exemption on a ~$70k stipend is roughly $10k. Arriving in July means the first calendar year is short.
- Indian residents may be able to claim the Article 21(2) standard deduction as "trainees," which nonresident aliens normally cannot. Eligibility for physicians on salaried employment is debated; advise a nonresident-tax preparer (Sprintax or the GME office's tool).
- Article 22 (teachers/researchers) generally does not fit clinical residents. Because of its retroactivity trap, do not claim it without professional advice.

## 3. Residency life

### Takeaway
PGY-1 pay averages about $68k nationally and about $77.6k in the Western region (AAMC, July 2025). J-1s cannot moonlight. A J-2 spouse can work with a $520 EAD (1–5 months processing; not to support the J-1). Travel requires a valid J-1 stamp; check DS-2019 and visa validity before any trip to India.

### Cited Findings
**Pay (AAMC 2025 survey, stipends as of 1 Jul 2025; report Nov 2025)**
- National unweighted PGY-1 mean is **$68,166**, up 2.2% year on year. The survey covered 350 institutions and 114,361 trainees. — [AMA, 22 Jan 2026](https://www.ama-assn.org/medical-residents/residency-life/resident-physician-pay-still-rising-growth-trails-inflation)
- Regional PGY-1 averages:

| Region | PGY-1 average |
|---|---|
| Northeast | $74,994 |
| Southern | $65,076 |
| Central | $68,580 |
| **Western** | **$77,649** |

  Source: [AMA](https://www.ama-assn.org/medical-residents/residency-life/resident-physician-pay-still-rising-growth-trails-inflation)

**Work rules**
- Moonlighting is prohibited on ECFMG J-1. — [ECFMG EVSP general](https://ecfmg.org/evsp/applying-general.html)

**J-2 spouse work authorization (EAD)** — [MIT ISchO, updated Feb 2026](https://ischo.mit.edu/information-daily-life/information-spouses-partners-and-families/j-2-work-permission)
- File Form I-765 (category c)(5) by mail; online filing is not available.
- The fee is **$520**, payable by credit card only since 30 Sep 2025.
- Processing takes about **1–5 months**.
- The EAD is valid for 1 year or the DS-2019 period, whichever is shorter.
- The spouse may not work until the card is issued, and the income may not be used to support the J-1. A signed statement is required.
- From 12 Dec 2025 USCIS stopped accepting self-submitted photos, so biometrics may be requested.
- Renewal can be filed only after the J-1's DS-2019 is extended.

**Dependents and travel**
- J-2 dependents are also subject to 212(e) and to the insurance minimums. — [ECFMG](https://ecfmg.org/evsp/applying-general.html)
- Re-entry needs a valid J-1 stamp. Avoid travel while a change of status is pending. — [Intealth pre-arrival](https://certification.ecfmg.org/evsp/applying-pre-arrival.html)
- If the D/S rule takes effect, every re-entry is admitted under the fixed-date rules, so renew the passport early. — [UTHSCSA](https://wp.uthscsa.edu/ois/immigration/ecfmg-j-1/elimination-of-duration-of-status-impact-on-ecfmg-j-1-alien-physicians/)

### Inferences
- Bay Area programs pay above the Western average; UCSF and Stanford PGY-1 salaries exceed $90k plus housing stipends. This was **not verified** here, so publish only after pulling the programs' own 2026–27 salary pages.
- Visa-stamp renewal means an interview in India. Given the 2026 environment, residents should avoid casual trips home if the stamp has expired, and plan any renewal trip months ahead with a program letter.

## 4. After residency

### Takeaway
Detailed research on this phase was not done in this pass. The binding constraints are already covered in the earlier report: the 212(e) two-year rule, the Conrad 30 waiver (California applications 1–31 Oct), the HHS waiver backlog, H-1B fee litigation and the EB-2 India backlog. The MoHFW bond adds an Indian-side consequence to not returning.

### Cited Findings
- J-1 physicians and J-2 dependents must complete a two-year home-country physical presence before certain status changes or adjustments, unless waived. — [ECFMG](https://ecfmg.org/evsp/applying-general.html)
- The MoHFW bond and undertaking (₹10 lakh plus 12% interest on default) is tied to the SoN. — [MoHFW OM 2023](https://mohfw.gov.in/sites/default/files/Revised%20guidelines%20for%20issuance%20of%20SON%2C%20ENC%20and%20NORI_0.pdf)
- After the first 2 calendar years, J-1 physicians generally become resident aliens for tax purposes and lose the FICA exemption. — [IRS](https://irs.gov/individuals/international-taxpayers/aliens-employed-in-the-u-s-social-security-taxes) (the exemption ends on becoming a resident alien)
- NRIs cannot remit under LRS. — [ICICI](https://www.icici.bank.in/nri-banking/nriedge/nri-articles/lrs-for-sending-money-abroad-from-india)

### Inferences
- Once she leaves India for employment she becomes an NRI under FEMA. Her Indian savings accounts should be redesignated as NRO, and she can open an NRE account for US earnings, which can be repatriated and whose interest is tax-free in India. This is standard practice but not cited here.
- OCI does not apply while she holds an Indian passport.
- How the MoHFW bond is enforced against waiver recipients in practice is unknown. Advise families that the sureties carry real exposure.

### A. Pre-departure

**Correction to pass 1 (travel signature still exists)**
- Re-entry still requires a DS-2019 with a travel signature in the "Travel Validation by Responsible Officer" box (Intealth page, updated 27 Feb 2026, [link](https://ecfmg.org/evsp/during-travel.html)):
  - The signature is valid for one year or until the DS-2019 end date (Box 3), whichever comes first. One signature covers every trip in that window.
  - Only Intealth Regional Advisors can sign. If you lack a valid travel-validated DS-2019, file a "Request for Duplicate Form DS-2019." Intealth processes it within 5 business days and posts it in MyIntealth. Get it before booking a visa appointment.
  - State Department security checks take about 5 business days to 3+ months.
  - For a visa renewal abroad, bring the DS-160, fees, photo, passport, a paper travel-validated DS-2019 and evidence of nonimmigrant intent. Hand-carry the documents; do not check them in baggage.
  - Automatic revalidation applies to trips of under 30 days to Canada, Mexico or the adjacent islands. It is not available if a new visa application is pending or was denied there.

**Fees**
- SEVIS I-901 fee (ICE, [link](https://ice.gov/sevis/i901)): **$220** for J exchange visitors (full payment), $35 for special J categories, $350 for F/M. The page shows no update date. The $220 J rate took effect 24 Jun 2019 ([BAL, 4 Jun 2019](https://www.bal.com/immigration-news/ice-to-increase-fees-on-f-and-m-students-j-exchange-visitors-among-others/)).
- Intealth J-1 sponsorship (EVSP) fee: **[unverified]**. The ecfmg.org fee pages returned errors. Programs often pay this fee; confirm with the GME office.
- MoHFW Statement of Need application fee and processing time: **[unverified]**. No official source found; pass 1 has only the ₹100 stamp paper.

**NPI and DEA**
- The NPI is **free**. Apply online through NPPES (nppes.cms.hhs.gov) in about 20 minutes, or on paper with Form CMS-10114. Update NPPES within 30 days of any change. — [CMS prescriber NPI fact sheet (c. 2013)](https://www.cms.gov/regulations-and-guidance/administrative-simplification/nationalprovidentstand/downloads/npi-requirements-for-prescribers.pdf)
  - The fact sheet does not mention residents. Programs usually ask for the NPI at onboarding, once the applicant has a US address and, ideally, an SSN **[unverified timing]**.
- DEA: a personal DEA registration is generally not needed during residency. Under 21 CFR 1301.22(c), a practitioner employed by a hospital may prescribe under the hospital's DEA registration with a hospital-assigned suffix (for example AP0123456-10), if the state permits it and the hospital verifies this. — [21 CFR 1301.22 (LII)](https://www.law.cornell.edu/cfr/text/21/1301.22)
  - Moonlighting is banned on J-1 anyway, so a personal DEA registration matters mainly after residency **[inference]**.

**TB testing and onboarding health checks**
- CDC/NTCA 2019 guidance for healthcare personnel ([MMWR 68(19)](https://www.cdc.gov/mmwr/volumes/68/wr/mm6819a3.htm)):
  - Pre-placement screening: an individual TB risk assessment, a symptom evaluation, and an IGRA or TST if there is no documented prior TB or LTBI.
  - A positive result in a low-risk person needs a second test.
  - Routine annual testing is not recommended without exposure, but annual TB education is.
  - New positives need a chest X-ray, and LTBI treatment is encouraged.
- **[Inference, not sourced]** BCG vaccination is near-universal in India, and BCG can cause false-positive TST results. Bring any prior IGRA (QuantiFERON) report, a recent chest X-ray if one was done, and vaccination records (MMR, varicella, hepatitis B with titers, Tdap, influenza, COVID). Drug screens, background checks and BLS/ACLS (often given at orientation) vary by institution; no official cross-program source was found.

### C. Taxes

- IRS Pub 519 (2025 returns), [link](https://www.irs.gov/publications/p519):
  - A "teacher or trainee" is "an individual, other than a student, who is temporarily in the United States under a 'J' or 'Q' visa" and substantially complies with it. Such a person is not exempt (that is, their days start counting toward the substantial-presence test) if they were exempt as a teacher, trainee or student for any part of **2 of the 6 preceding calendar years**.
  - Students get 5 calendar years instead.
  - Form 8843 must be filed for each exempt year. Attach it to Form 1040-NR, or mail it alone to the IRS in Austin, TX 73301-0215, by the 1040-NR due date.
  - Pub 519 does not mention physicians by name.
  - **Inference (well supported):** J-1 alien physicians are not students, so they fall under "teacher or trainee." That means nonresident status, 1040-NR and FICA exemption for the first 2 calendar years. The July arrival year counts as year 1.
- FICA: Pub 519 states that services by a nonresident alien temporarily in the US in F, J, M or Q status are generally not covered by Social Security if they are performed to carry out the purpose of admission. It does not list J sub-categories, so it applies to J-1 physicians while they are nonresident aliens. — [IRS Pub 519](https://www.irs.gov/publications/p519); see also pass 1 (IRS FICA page).
- India treaty: Pub 519 says eligible "students and business apprentices from India" under **Article 21(2)** may claim the standard deduction, and may claim an extra withholding allowance for it, using Worksheet 5-1. — [IRS Pub 519](https://www.irs.gov/publications/p519)
  - Whether a salaried resident physician counts as a "business apprentice" is **[unverified]**.
  - Articles 15/16 (independent/dependent personal services) were not researched against residents' wages. Do not publish a conclusion without a tax professional.
- **Still open:** state tax treatment (California does not generally conform to federal treaty benefits; this was not fetched) and W-4 nonresident instructions (Notice 1392).
