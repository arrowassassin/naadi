# 14. Step 3 and licensure

*Stage key `step3_and_licensure` · last verified 2026-11-15 · re-verify quarterly and immediately on policy news.*

Step 3 requires a valid ECFMG certificate and is offered only in the US and territories [USMLE](https://www.usmle.org/bulletin-information/eligibility) [Careers360](https://studyabroad.careers360.com/articles/usmle-test-centres). In California, IMG residents must hold a Postgraduate Training License within 180 days of starting and can get a full license after 24 months of approved training, with Step 3 required [MBC](https://www.mbc.ca.gov/Licensing/Physicians-and-Surgeons/Apply/) [UC Davis GME](https://health.ucdavis.edu/gme/camb_licensing.html). California requires all parts of Step 3 within four attempts [B&P 2177](https://california.public.law/codes/business_and_professions_code_section_2177). Take Step 3 in PGY-1 unless you are pursuing an H-1B for residency.

## Task details

**Check Step 3 eligibility** (`s3lic-01`) — IMGs need Step 1 and Step 2 CK passes plus ECFMG certification with a valid, unexpired certificate; completing or nearing one US postgraduate year is recommended, not required [USMLE](https://www.usmle.org/bulletin-information/eligibility). Step 3 is offered only in the US and territories [Careers360](https://studyabroad.careers360.com/articles/usmle-test-centres). The fee (about $955) is unverified.

**Schedule Step 3 during PGY-1** (`s3lic-02`) — The 2025 IMG first-time Step 3 pass rate was 88% [USMLE](https://www.usmle.org/performance-data). California requires Step 3 for the full license [UC Davis GME](https://health.ucdavis.edu/gme/camb_licensing.html), and an H-1B for post-waiver work generally requires it (inference). PGY-1 is the recommended window.

**H-1B strategy only: consider Step 3 before ERAS** (`s3lic-03`) — An H-1B for residency generally needs Step 3 passed before the petition [FSMB/attorneys](https://www.fsmb.org/siteassets/baw-speaker-presentation/session-8-imgs-fsmb-attorneys-nov-2025.pdf). Naadi's judgment: attempt it before ERAS only if your Step 2 CK is about 250+, you are ECFMG-certified by mid-year, and you have named H-1B programs you would rank; it costs about $955 (unverified) plus a US stay.

**Respect California's four-attempt Step 3 rule** (`s3lic-04`) — California requires passing all parts of Step 3 “within not more than four attempts”; applicants who needed more may still be considered under B&P §2135 or §2135.5 [B&P 2177](https://california.public.law/codes/business_and_professions_code_section_2177).

**California: obtain your PTL within 180 days** (`s3lic-05`) — California ACGME trainees must obtain a Postgraduate Training License within 180 days of their program start date [MBC](https://www.mbc.ca.gov/Licensing/Physicians-and-Surgeons/Apply/). The PTL is valid 36 months; deadlines are statutory, “not flexible”, and missing one removes you from clinical duties [UC Davis GME](https://health.ucdavis.edu/gme/camb_licensing.html). The PTL fee was not re-verified.

**California: apply for the full P&S license after 24 months** (`s3lic-06`) — IMGs can move from the PTL to a full Physician's and Surgeon's license after 24 months of Board-approved training (12 for US/Canadian graduates), and Step 3 is required [MBC](https://www.mbc.ca.gov/Licensing/Physicians-and-Surgeons/Apply/) [UC Davis GME](https://health.ucdavis.edu/gme/camb_licensing.html).

**New York: know the full-license bar** (`s3lic-07`) — A full New York license for IMGs needs 3 years of accredited GME, Steps 1–3, a $735 fee and FCVS [NYSED](https://www.op.nysed.gov/professions/physicians/license-requirements). A limited permit ($105) is needed only outside ACGME residency, valid 2 years and renewable once [NYSED](https://www.op.nysed.gov/professions/physicians/license-requirements).

**Permanently validate your Pathway-based ECFMG certificate** (`s3lic-08`) — A Pathway-based certificate expires with its Pathway unless revalidated or permanently validated; 12 months of ACGME-accredited training permanently validates it [ECFMG](https://ecfmg.org/certification-pathways/expiration.html). Confirm validation shows in MyIntealth after PGY-1.

**Do not count on state alternative licensure pathways** (`s3lic-09`) — California's AB 2386 died in Senate Appropriations on 13 Aug 2026 [CalMatters](https://calmatters.digitaldemocracy.org/bills/ca_202520260ab2386). The pathways enacted elsewhere (18 states per FSMB's Oct 2025 chart) require prior licensed practice or postgraduate training abroad, so none help a fresh MBBS graduate [FSMB](https://inte.fsmb.org/siteassets/advocacy/policies/states-with-enacted-and-proposed-additional-img-licensure-pathways-key-issue-chart.pdf) [WES](https://www.wes.org/resource-library/blog/welcoming-communities-in-action/27-states-expand-pathways-for-internationally-trained-physicians-in-2026/).

## California licensure summary

PTL within 180 days of start; PTL valid 36 months; full Physician's and Surgeon's license after 24 months of approved training for IMGs; Step 3 required [MBC](https://www.mbc.ca.gov/Licensing/Physicians-and-Surgeons/Apply/) [UC Davis GME](https://health.ucdavis.edu/gme/camb_licensing.html). Step 3 must be passed within 4 attempts [B&P 2177](https://california.public.law/codes/business_and_professions_code_section_2177). AB 2386 died 13 Aug 2026 [CalMatters](https://calmatters.digitaldemocracy.org/bills/ca_202520260ab2386).

## Common mistakes

- Missing California's 180-day PTL deadline — removal from clinical duties [UC Davis GME](https://health.ucdavis.edu/gme/camb_licensing.html).
- Exceeding four Step 3 attempts if you want a California license [B&P 2177](https://california.public.law/codes/business_and_professions_code_section_2177).
- Banking on state alternative pathways [FSMB](https://inte.fsmb.org/siteassets/advocacy/policies/states-with-enacted-and-proposed-additional-img-licensure-pathways-key-issue-chart.pdf).
