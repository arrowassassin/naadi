# 12. Arrival: first 30 days

*Stage key `arrival_first_30_days` · last verified 2026-11-15 · re-verify quarterly and immediately on policy news.*

At the airport check that your I-94 reads “J-1 D/S”, then report to the program so the Training Program Liaison validates your arrival in SEVIS [Intealth](https://certification.ecfmg.org/evsp/applying-pre-arrival.html). Apply for an SSN (free) after waiting 11 business days from SEVIS validation [Intealth](https://certification.ecfmg.org/evsp/applying-pre-arrival.html) [SSA](https://www.ssa.gov/pubs/EN-05-10096.pdf). Then: bank account and secured card [Capital One](https://www.capitalone.com/learn-grow/money-management/how-can-immigrants-build-credit), driver license [CA DMV](https://www.dmv.ca.gov/portal/driver-licenses-identification-cards/driver-licenses-dl/), nonresident tax setup [IRS](https://www.irs.gov/publications/p519), and a J-2 EAD if your spouse will work ($520) [MIT](https://ischo.mit.edu/information-daily-life/information-spouses-partners-and-families/j-2-work-permission). Watch the postponed DHS rule replacing D/S with fixed admission dates [UTHSCSA OIS](https://wp.uthscsa.edu/ois/immigration/ecfmg-j-1/elimination-of-duration-of-status-impact-on-ecfmg-j-1-alien-physicians/).

## Task details

**Check your I-94 at entry** (`arr-01`) — At CBP present passport, visa and DS-2019; the I-94 should read “J-1 D/S” [Intealth](https://certification.ecfmg.org/evsp/applying-pre-arrival.html). If it shows a fixed date or a different status, contact Intealth immediately, and print the electronic I-94 [Intealth](https://certification.ecfmg.org/evsp/applying-pre-arrival.html).

**Report to your program for SEVIS validation** (`arr-02`) — The TPL submits the arrival form plus copies of your visa and I-94 in MyIntealth, and Intealth validates you in SEVIS “as soon as possible” [Intealth](https://certification.ecfmg.org/evsp/applying-pre-arrival.html). After validation the program start date cannot be amended [Intealth](https://certification.ecfmg.org/evsp/applying-pre-arrival.html).

**Report your US address in MyIntealth** (`arr-03`) — Report address changes within 10 days through MyIntealth [Intealth](https://certification.ecfmg.org/evsp/applying-pre-arrival.html). Do it again every time you move.

**Apply for your Social Security Number** (`arr-04`) — Intealth says wait 11 business days after SEVIS validation and select “other” for citizenship [Intealth](https://certification.ecfmg.org/evsp/applying-pre-arrival.html); SSA says wait 10 days after arrival [SSA](https://www.ssa.gov/pubs/EN-05-10096.pdf). Start online, visit an SSA office within 45 days, bring originals of your DS-2019, I-94 and passport (no photocopies); the service is free [SSA](https://www.ssa.gov/pubs/EN-05-10096.pdf). USCIS paused Enumeration Beyond Entry in April 2025, so apply directly to SSA [MIT ISchO](https://ischo.mit.edu/information-daily-life/information-spouses-partners-and-families/j-2-work-permission).

**Open a bank account and build credit** (`arr-05`) — Foreign credit history does not transfer; secured cards need a refundable deposit, e.g. Capital One Platinum Secured takes $49, $99 or $200 for a line of at least $200 [Capital One](https://www.capitalone.com/learn-grow/money-management/how-can-immigrants-build-credit). Issuers usually ask for an SSN, and the FTC warns against paid “tradeline renting” [Capital One](https://www.capitalone.com/learn-grow/money-management/how-can-immigrants-build-credit).

**Get a state driver license (California example)** (`arr-06`) — California needs an online application, an office visit (identity, residency documents, thumbprint, vision, photo), a knowledge test and a drive test, 3 attempts each; bring proof of insurance and an accompanying licensed driver if you hold a foreign license [CA DMV](https://www.dmv.ca.gov/portal/driver-licenses-identification-cards/driver-licenses-dl/). If your legal presence ends within 5 years you get a limited-term license [CA DMV](https://www.dmv.ca.gov/portal/driver-licenses-identification-cards/limited-term-for-legal-presence/). Plan to get it promptly once you are a resident (inference).

**Set up nonresident tax status and your W-4** (`arr-07`) — A J-1 “teacher or trainee” is exempt from the substantial-presence count unless exempt for any part of 2 of the 6 preceding calendar years, and files Form 8843 each exempt year [IRS Pub 519](https://www.irs.gov/publications/p519). So J-1 physicians are generally nonresident aliens filing 1040-NR for the first 2 calendar years, with the July arrival year counting as year 1 (well-supported inference, verify). Use a nonresident tax preparer; W-4 nonresident instructions were not sourced.

**Check your payslip for the FICA exemption** (`arr-08`) — Nonresident aliens in J status are exempt from Social Security and Medicare tax on wages for services that carry out the purpose of admission; the exemption ends when you become a resident alien for tax purposes and does not apply to J-2s [IRS](https://irs.gov/individuals/international-taxpayers/aliens-employed-in-the-u-s-social-security-taxes) [IRS Pub 519](https://www.irs.gov/publications/p519). FICA is 7.65%, roughly $10k over two years on a ~$70k stipend (inference).

**Ask a preparer about the India treaty standard deduction** (`arr-09`) — Under Article 21(2), eligible students and business apprentices from India may claim the standard deduction [IRS Pub 519](https://www.irs.gov/publications/p519). Whether a salaried resident physician counts is unverified, and Article 22 (teachers/researchers) carries a retroactive trap [Sprintax](https://blog.sprintax.com/us-india-tax-treaty/); do not claim either without professional advice.

**File the J-2 spouse's work permit (EAD)** (`arr-10`) — File Form I-765, category (c)(5), by mail; the fee is $520 by credit card, processing about 1–5 months, valid 1 year or the DS-2019 period [MIT ISchO](https://ischo.mit.edu/information-daily-life/information-spouses-partners-and-families/j-2-work-permission). The spouse may not work until the card arrives, and the income may not support the J-1 holder; a signed statement is required [MIT ISchO](https://ischo.mit.edu/information-daily-life/information-spouses-partners-and-families/j-2-work-permission).

**Enroll children in public school** (`arr-11`) — Under Plyler v. Doe, a student's or parent's immigration status cannot be used to bar a child from public school [US Dept of Education](https://www.ed.gov/laws-and-policy/civil-rights-laws/title-vi/title-vi-key-issues/equal-rights-public-education-regardless-of-immigrationcitizenship-status). Districts may ask for proof of residency and age.

**Watch the fixed admission-period (D/S) rule** (`arr-12`) — A DHS final rule published 17 Jul 2026 replaces D/S with a fixed Admit Until Date (program end plus 30 days, maximum 4 years per admission); extensions would need Form I-539 or travel and re-entry [UTHSCSA OIS](https://wp.uthscsa.edu/ois/immigration/ecfmg-j-1/elimination-of-duration-of-status-impact-on-ecfmg-j-1-alien-physicians/). Due 15 Sep 2026, it was postponed with no new date as of 23 Sep 2026; physicians already in D/S generally see no immediate change [UTHSCSA OIS](https://wp.uthscsa.edu/ois/immigration/ecfmg-j-1/elimination-of-duration-of-status-impact-on-ecfmg-j-1-alien-physicians/) [Klasko](https://www.klaskolaw.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/2026.09.09-Presentation-Duration-of-Status-Eliminated_Navigating-the-New-Fixed-Admission-Period_Healthcare-RG-TD-IW.pdf).

## Fixed admission-period rule (watch)

Published 17 Jul 2026; would replace D/S with an Admit Until Date (program end + 30 days, max 4 years per admission); postponed with no new date as of 23 Sep 2026 [UTHSCSA OIS](https://wp.uthscsa.edu/ois/immigration/ecfmg-j-1/elimination-of-duration-of-status-impact-on-ecfmg-j-1-alien-physicians/) [Klasko](https://www.klaskolaw.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/2026.09.09-Presentation-Duration-of-Status-Eliminated_Navigating-the-New-Fixed-Admission-Period_Healthcare-RG-TD-IW.pdf).

## Common mistakes

- Not checking the I-94 for “J-1 D/S” at the airport [Intealth](https://certification.ecfmg.org/evsp/applying-pre-arrival.html).
- Applying for the SSN before SEVIS validation has settled [Intealth](https://certification.ecfmg.org/evsp/applying-pre-arrival.html).
- Bringing photocopies to SSA — originals only [SSA](https://www.ssa.gov/pubs/EN-05-10096.pdf).
- Letting a J-2 spouse work before the EAD arrives [MIT](https://ischo.mit.edu/information-daily-life/information-spouses-partners-and-families/j-2-work-permission).
- Paying for “tradeline” credit boosts [Capital One](https://www.capitalone.com/learn-grow/money-management/how-can-immigrants-build-credit).
- Claiming India treaty Article 22 without professional advice [Sprintax](https://blog.sprintax.com/us-india-tax-treaty/).
