# 01. Eligibility and ECFMG application

*Stage key `eligibility` · last verified 2026-11-15 · re-verify quarterly and immediately on policy news.*

Before any exam, confirm your medical school is listed in WDOMS with an ECFMG Sponsor Note that covers your graduation year; with no Sponsor Note you are not eligible [ECFMG](https://ecfmg.org/2026ib/certification-requirements.html). Open a MyIntealth account ($110) and submit the Application for ECFMG Certification ($580) plus diploma and transcript verification ($110 each) [IMGPrep](https://www.imgprep.com/ecfmg-certification-cost). Do this first: a 2027 Pathway application requires your ECFMG application to be “Accepted” or “Pending Credential Verification”, and your OET booking must carry your MyIntealth ID [ECFMG Pathways](https://apps.wdoms.org/certification-pathways/) [2027 FAQ](https://apps.wdoms.org/certification-pathways/faqs.html). Since January 2026 you register for USMLE through FSMB, not ECFMG [ECFMG News](https://www.ecfmg.org/news/category/pathways/).

## Task details

**Verify your school's WDOMS Sponsor Note** (`elig-01`) — ECFMG requires your school to be listed in the World Directory of Medical Schools with an ECFMG Sponsor Note that includes your graduation year; with no Sponsor Note you are not eligible [ECFMG](https://ecfmg.org/2026ib/certification-requirements.html). Search your college on WDOMS and compare the exact institution name and graduation year on your diploma (college vs parent-university names can differ) [WDOMS](https://apps.wdoms.org/resources/sponsor-notes.html). School IDs and year ranges quoted by coaching sites or AI roadmaps have not been independently verified, so check them yourself (verify).

**Confirm basic ECFMG eligibility** (`elig-02`) — You need credit for at least 4 academic years and must hold your final medical diploma [ECFMG](https://ecfmg.org/2026ib/certification-requirements.html). USMLE Step 1 and Step 2 CK also require a school listed in WDOMS as meeting ECFMG eligibility requirements [USMLE](https://www.usmle.org/bulletin-information/eligibility).

**Check passport validity** (`elig-03`) — Your J-1 interview will require a passport valid at least 6 months beyond the DS-2019 end date [Intealth](https://certification.ecfmg.org/evsp/applying-pre-arrival.html). If yours would expire within 6 months of your expected training end date, renew it now, before the passport number is entered into MyIntealth, FSMB, OET and visa records.

**Open a MyIntealth account** (`elig-04`) — The MyIntealth account costs $110, which includes a $50 NotaryCam notarization [IMGPrep](https://www.imgprep.com/ecfmg-certification-cost). Your OET booking must carry your MyIntealth ID so results reach ECFMG [2027 FAQ](https://apps.wdoms.org/certification-pathways/faqs.html). Expect lead time for the identity and notarization steps (timing inferred, verify).

**Submit the Application for ECFMG Certification** (`elig-05`) — The application fee is $580 (about ₹51,000) [IMGPrep](https://www.imgprep.com/ecfmg-certification-cost). To apply for a 2027 Pathway by 31 Jan 2027, this application must already be “Accepted” or “Pending Credential Verification” in MyIntealth [ECFMG](https://apps.wdoms.org/certification-pathways/). Submit it in your first week so the Pathway window stays open.

**Upload diploma and transcript for verification** (`elig-06`) — Diploma and transcript verification cost $110 each [IMGPrep](https://www.imgprep.com/ecfmg-certification-cost). All credentials must be uploaded via MyIntealth; copies sent by mail, courier or email are not accepted [ECFMG](https://ecfmg.org/2026ib/). ECFMG verifies the diploma directly with your school and requests the final transcript from it [ECFMG](https://ecfmg.org/2026ib/certification-requirements.html).

**Alert your college registrar to the verification request** (`elig-07`) — ECFMG performs primary-source verification with the school itself [ECFMG](https://ecfmg.org/2026ib/certification-requirements.html). No sourced data exists on how quickly Indian universities answer these requests (verify), so tell the registrar or dean's office to expect it and follow up weekly until it is returned.

**Register for USMLE through FSMB, not ECFMG** (`elig-08`) — USMLE services moved from ECFMG to FSMB in January 2026; MyIntealth still handles credentials [ECFMG News](https://www.ecfmg.org/news/category/pathways/). Older guides telling you to apply for Steps inside ECFMG's system are out of date.

**Check whether you qualify for Pathway 1** (`elig-09`) — Pathway 1 needs a license or registration to practise without supervision that was valid on or after 1 Jan 2022; it need not be current, but supervised, training or restricted licenses do not count [ECFMG Pathway 1](https://corp.ecfmg.org/certification-pathways/pathway-1.html). ECFMG has not stated whether Indian NMC or State Medical Council permanent registration counts, so email ECFMG to confirm before paying (verify).

**Check whether you qualify for Pathway 3** (`elig-10`) — Pathway 3 requires graduation on or after 1 Jan 2024 from a school accredited by a WFME-recognized agency, and a school official must attest to your clinical skills [ECFMG Pathways 3–5](https://certification.ecfmg.org/certification-pathways/pathways-3-5.html). Check that your college appears on ECFMG's “Medical Schools Eligible for 2027 Pathways” list [ECFMG](https://certification.ecfmg.org/certification-pathways/pathways-3-5.html).

**Request licensing documents from the issuing authority** (`elig-11`) — For Pathways, licensing documents must come directly from the issuing authority by email or post/courier [ECFMG FAQ](https://ecfmg.org/certification-pathways/faqs.html). Some guides say good-standing documents must be issued within 90 days; that wording is unverified (verify with ECFMG).

**Note the 7-year exam window and attempt limits** (`elig-12`) — All exams for ECFMG certification must be passed within seven years of the first exam passed, and ECFMG sends no reminders [ECFMG](https://ecfmg.org/2026ib/time-limit-complete-exams.html). You may take a Step at most 3 times in 12 months; 4 failed attempts at any Step make you ineligible for USMLE [USMLE](https://www.usmle.org/bulletin-information/eligibility).

## ECFMG certification: the four parts

1. Accepted application with diploma and transcript verified directly with your school via MyIntealth.
2. Step 1 pass.
3. Step 2 CK pass.
4. An accepted, unexpired Pathway, which includes OET Medicine.

Source: [ECFMG 2026 IB](https://ecfmg.org/2026ib/certification-requirements.html)

## Common mistakes

- Trusting a school ID or Sponsor Note year range from a coaching site instead of checking WDOMS yourself [WDOMS](https://apps.wdoms.org/resources/sponsor-notes.html).
- Leaving the ECFMG application until after Step 1 — a 2027 Pathway needs it “Accepted” or “Pending Credential Verification” by 31 Jan 2027 [ECFMG](https://apps.wdoms.org/certification-pathways/).
- Mailing or emailing documents — only MyIntealth uploads are accepted [ECFMG](https://ecfmg.org/2026ib/).
- Following pre-2026 guides that register USMLE through ECFMG; it is FSMB now [ECFMG News](https://www.ecfmg.org/news/category/pathways/).
- Assuming Indian permanent registration automatically qualifies for Pathway 1 without asking ECFMG (verify).
