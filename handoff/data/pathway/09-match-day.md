# 09. Match week

*Stage key `match_day` · last verified 2026-11-15 · re-verify quarterly and immediately on policy news.*

In the 2027 Match, Match status is released 15 Mar, SOAP runs 15–18 Mar and Match Day is 19 Mar [NRMP](https://www.nrmp.org/match-calendars/main-residency-applicants/); 2028 dates are not yet published. The week after Match starts the visa critical path: contract, MoHFW Statement of Need, Intealth J-1 application, DS-2019 [Intealth](https://certification.ecfmg.org/evsp/applying-pre-arrival.html) [CGI San Francisco](https://www.cgisf.gov.in/page/statement-of-need-certificates-for-medical-doctors/).

## Task details

**Check your Match status on Monday** (`match-01`) — In the 2027 Match, Match status is released 15 Mar 2027 [NRMP](https://www.nrmp.org/match-calendars/main-residency-applicants/). This tells you whether you matched, not where.

**If unmatched, enter SOAP** (`match-02`) — SOAP runs 15–18 Mar in the 2027 Match [NRMP](https://www.nrmp.org/match-calendars/main-residency-applicants/). Have your ERAS documents ready and filter offers for J-1 sponsorship before accepting.

**Match Day** (`match-03`) — Match Day is 19 Mar 2027 for the 2027 Match [NRMP](https://www.nrmp.org/match-calendars/main-residency-applicants/); 2028's (likely Friday 17 Mar) is inferred. In 2026, 42.3% of non-US IMGs matched to their first-choice program [NRMP 2026](https://www.nrmp.org/wp-content/uploads/2026/05/Main_Match_Results_and_Data-2026.pdf).

**Sign the contract or offer letter** (`match-04`) — Your contract or offer letter is part of the financial evidence for the J-1 interview [Intealth](https://certification.ecfmg.org/evsp/applying-pre-arrival.html). Sign and return it promptly and keep a PDF in your document vault.

**Ask the program to start your Intealth J-1 application** (`match-05`) — The program's Training Program Liaison (TPL) initiates the Intealth J-1 application and receives the DS-2019 with you in MyIntealth [Intealth](https://certification.ecfmg.org/evsp/applying-pre-arrival.html). Tell the coordinator early if your visa interview may fall after mid-June.

**Ask GME which state training license you need** (`match-06`) — Rules differ: ACGME residents in New York need no license [NYSED](https://www.op.nysed.gov/professions/physicians/license-requirements), Texas requires a Physician-in-Training permit [TMB](https://www.tmb.texas.gov/apply-renew/physician/physician-apply/physician-in-training-permit-application), Illinois a temporary license that takes 6–8 weeks [Rush GME](https://www.rushu.rush.edu/sites/default/files/2026-02/Initial%20Temporary%20Medical%20License%20Checklist__with%20FAQ_2026.pdf), and California a PTL within 180 days of starting [MBC](https://www.mbc.ca.gov/Licensing/Physicians-and-Surgeons/Apply/).

**If you did not match, plan the next cycle now** (`match-07`) — Many IMG-friendly programs filter at “graduated within 5 years” [FREIDA](https://freida.ama-assn.org/program/1400531051). Use the year for a higher-yield profile: more US exposure, Step 3 if eligible, and documented clinical work in India.

## The post-Match critical path

Contract → MoHFW Statement of Need (₹10 lakh bond, two sureties) [MoHFW](https://mohfw.gov.in/sites/default/files/Revised%20guidelines%20for%20issuance%20of%20SON%2C%20ENC%20and%20NORI_0.pdf) → Intealth J-1 application (TPL-initiated) → DS-2019 in MyIntealth → SEVIS I-901 ($220) → DS-160 ($185) → J-1 interview in India → possible administrative processing → enter no earlier than 30 days before start [Intealth](https://certification.ecfmg.org/evsp/applying-pre-arrival.html). Allow 6–10 weeks plus a cushion (inference).

## Common mistakes

- Waiting until April to start visa paperwork.
- Accepting a SOAP offer without checking J-1 sponsorship.
- Not telling the program early about a late visa interview [Intealth](https://certification.ecfmg.org/evsp/applying-pre-arrival.html).
