# 02. USMLE Step 1

*Stage key `step1` · last verified 2026-11-15 · re-verify quarterly and immediately on policy news.*

Step 1 is pass/fail, now 14 × 30-minute blocks (280 items) [Lecturio](https://www.lecturio.com/blog/2026-usmle-changes-step-1-2-ck-software-block-updates/), and costs $695 + $210 international surcharge when taken in India [IMGPrep](https://www.imgprep.com/ecfmg-certification-cost). The 2025 first-time pass rate for IMGs was 75% [USMLE](https://www.usmle.org/performance-data). You get at most 3 attempts at a Step in 12 months and 4 failed attempts end USMLE eligibility, so sit only when ready [USMLE](https://www.usmle.org/bulletin-information/eligibility). A NEET PG year gives strong recall; the job is retraining for mechanism-based, two-step questions using UWorld and NBME self-assessments.

## Task details

**Apply for Step 1 in the FSMB portal** (`s1-01`) — The 2026 fee for testing in India is $695 plus a $210 international surcharge, about ₹80,500 [IMGPrep](https://www.imgprep.com/ecfmg-certification-cost). Whether 18% GST applies, and the exact eligibility-period length, are unverified (verify on FSMB). Pick an eligibility period that covers your target month plus a few weeks of slack.

**Book your Prometric seat as soon as the permit arrives** (`s1-02`) — Indian Prometric USMLE centres are listed in Ahmedabad, Prayagraj, Bengaluru, Chennai, Hyderabad, Kolkata, Mumbai and Gurugram [Careers360](https://studyabroad.careers360.com/articles/usmle-test-centres). Application approval does not reserve a seat and preferred centres may be full, so search several cities [Careers360](https://studyabroad.careers360.com/articles/usmle-test-centres).

**Buy UWorld Step 1** (`s1-03`) — UWorld Step 1 costs $459 for 90 days with one self-assessment, or $519 for 180 days with two (about ₹41–46k) [UWorld](https://medical.uworld.com/usmle/usmle-step-1/). Choose the 180-day plan if your exam date might slip.

**Take a baseline NBME CBSSA** (`s1-04`) — NBME sells CBSSA forms for Step 1 through MyNBME [NBME](https://www.nbme.org/examinees/self-assessments); a Sept 2025 article put them at $60 each [Elite Medical Prep](https://elitemedicalprep.com/nbme-25-nbme-30-best-predictor-for-passing-step-1/). Take one early to see where your NEET PG knowledge stands against USMLE-style questions.

**Run UWorld timed and random every day** (`s1-05`) — The plan is about 80–120 UWorld questions a day, timed and random by week 2, with careful review [UWorld](https://medical.uworld.com/usmle/usmle-step-1/). Use free Mehlman HY PDFs [TestPrepPal](https://testpreppal.com/usmle/cheat-sheets/mehlman-medical) and Divine Intervention podcasts [Divine](https://divineinterventionpodcasts.com/category/usmle-step-1/organ-systems-usmle-step-1/) only for weak areas.

**Schedule NBME forms every 1–2 weeks** (`s1-06`) — Informal, student-reported data describe a CBSSA of about 62–68 as the “low passing range” and about 70 as a safer bar [Elite Medical Prep](https://elitemedicalprep.com/nbme-25-nbme-30-best-predictor-for-passing-step-1/). Sit only after two fresh forms land there. Plan 3–4 forms in total.

**Make the go / no-go decision** (`s1-07`) — A failed attempt is permanent on your record, and many programs screen for first-attempt passes; a passed Step cannot be retaken [USMLE](https://www.usmle.org/bulletin-information/eligibility). If your last two forms are below range, move the exam by up to 4 weeks rather than risk a fail.

**Take the Free 120 3–7 days before the exam** (`s1-08`) — USMLE publishes free sample questions for each Step [USMLE](https://www.usmle.org/prepare-your-exam). Whether the “Free 120” name and format changed under the 2026 software is unverified (verify).

**Know the test-day format** (`s1-09`) — From 14 May 2026 Step 1 is reported as 14 blocks of 30 minutes, 20 questions each, 280 items in an 8-hour day [Lecturio](https://www.lecturio.com/blog/2026-usmle-changes-step-1-2-ck-software-block-updates/) (secondary; verify on usmle.org). Practise full-length days at this block length.

**Avoid pirated material and exam recalls** (`s1-10`) — Using exam “recalls” risks breaking USMLE rules, and NBME has publicly disclosed cheating cases [MDedge](https://www.mdedge.com/content/us-board-discloses-cheating-grads-say-problem-rampant/page/0/2). Pirated First Aid or UWorld PDFs circulating on Telegram also infringe copyright.

**Vet any coaching programme before paying** (`s1-11`) — No coaching provider publishes an independently verified match rate; one common “top centres in India” ranking is published by the company it ranks first [Mendel](https://mendel-website.vercel.app/blog/top-6-best-usmle-coaching-centers-in-india-for-step-1-step-2-CK-step-3-residency-match). Treat “guaranteed results” [example](https://sites.google.com/view/gateiit/join-leading-usmle-coaching-in-india-for-guaranteed-results) and success rates without a denominator [example](https://academically.com/blogs/img-match-rate-2026-can-indian-mbbs-doctors-actually-get-us-residency/) as red flags. If scores stall, hourly tutoring is an option; Mehlman packages range $185–$5,160 [TestPrepPal](https://testpreppal.com/usmle/cheat-sheets/mehlman-medical).

**Record your result and update the plan** (`s1-12`) — The 2025 first-time Step 1 pass rate was 75% for IMGs vs 93% for US MD students [USMLE](https://www.usmle.org/performance-data). Score reporting typically takes about 3–4 weeks (unverified). A pass unlocks most observership applications.

## Coaching red flags

“Guaranteed” results [example](https://sites.google.com/view/gateiit/join-leading-usmle-coaching-in-india-for-guaranteed-results); rankings written by the ranked company [example](https://mendel-website.vercel.app/blog/top-6-best-usmle-coaching-centers-in-india-for-step-1-step-2-CK-step-3-residency-match); success rates with no denominator or year [example](https://academically.com/blogs/img-match-rate-2026-can-indian-mbbs-doctors-actually-get-us-residency/); exam pass rates presented as match rates; letters promised before a rotation; “hands-on” work for a graduate on a B visa; payment into personal accounts; visa “guidance” from non-attorneys. No company can carry you “through the visa”: J-1 sponsorship comes from ECFMG/Intealth and H-1B from the hiring program.

## Common mistakes

- Sitting on a calendar date instead of readiness — a fail is permanent and a pass cannot be retaken [USMLE](https://www.usmle.org/bulletin-information/eligibility).
- Assuming application approval reserves a Prometric seat; it does not [Careers360](https://studyabroad.careers360.com/articles/usmle-test-centres).
- Buying every lecture series; spend on questions and self-assessments [UWorld](https://medical.uworld.com/usmle/usmle-step-1/) [NBME](https://www.nbme.org/examinees/self-assessments).
- Using “recalls” or pirated PDFs [MDedge](https://www.mdedge.com/content/us-board-discloses-cheating-grads-say-problem-rampant/page/0/2).
- Paying for coaching on the strength of self-published rankings [Mendel](https://mendel-website.vercel.app/blog/top-6-best-usmle-coaching-centers-in-india-for-step-1-step-2-CK-step-3-residency-match).
