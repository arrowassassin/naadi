# 11. Pre-departure

*Stage key `pre_departure` · last verified 2026-11-15 · re-verify quarterly and immediately on policy news.*

Between visa issuance and the flight: buy J-1-compliant insurance starting on the DS-2019 start date [Intealth](https://certification.ecfmg.org/evsp/applying-pre-arrival.html), secure the state training license your program needs [TMB](https://www.tmb.texas.gov/apply-renew/physician/physician-apply/physician-in-training-permit-application) [Rush GME](https://www.rushu.rush.edu/sites/default/files/2026-02/Initial%20Temporary%20Medical%20License%20Checklist__with%20FAQ_2026.pdf), complete TB screening [CDC](https://www.cdc.gov/mmwr/volumes/68/wr/mm6819a3.htm), plan money transfers under LRS [ICICI](https://www.icici.bank.in/nri-banking/nriedge/nri-articles/lrs-for-sending-money-abroad-from-india), and pack a hand-carry document folder [Intealth](https://ecfmg.org/evsp/during-travel.html). You may enter the US no earlier than 30 days before the DS-2019 start date [Intealth](https://certification.ecfmg.org/evsp/applying-pre-arrival.html).

## Task details

**Buy J-1-compliant health insurance** (`predep-01`) — Coverage must be at least $100,000 per accident or illness, with a deductible of no more than $500 and co-insurance of no more than 25%, for you and any J-2s; ECFMG provides repatriation ($25,000) and medical evacuation ($50,000) cover [ECFMG EVSP](https://ecfmg.org/evsp/applying-general.html). Insurance must start on the DS-2019 program start date and continue without a break [Intealth](https://certification.ecfmg.org/evsp/applying-pre-arrival.html). Gap-period cost was not sourced.

**Get the state training license your program needs** (`predep-02`) — New York: ACGME residents need no license [NYSED](https://www.op.nysed.gov/professions/physicians/license-requirements). Texas: Physician-in-Training permit, $200 + $7 surcharge, 14 business days for simple files to 90–120 days with formal review, applied for after the GME office gives you a TMB ID [TMB](https://www.tmb.texas.gov/apply-renew/physician/physician-apply/physician-in-training-permit-application). Illinois: temporary license $230, 6–8 weeks, and training cannot start until it is issued [Rush GME](https://www.rushu.rush.edu/sites/default/files/2026-02/Initial%20Temporary%20Medical%20License%20Checklist__with%20FAQ_2026.pdf). California: see the PTL task in Step 3 and licensure.

**Complete TB screening** (`predep-03`) — Pre-placement screening for healthcare personnel is a TB risk assessment, a symptom evaluation, and an IGRA or TST if you have no documented prior TB or LTBI; new positives need a chest X-ray [CDC MMWR](https://www.cdc.gov/mmwr/volumes/68/wr/mm6819a3.htm). BCG, near-universal in India, can cause false-positive TSTs, so bring any prior IGRA (QuantiFERON) report (inference, not sourced).

**Gather vaccination and onboarding records** (`predep-04`) — Bring records for MMR, varicella, hepatitis B with titers, Tdap, influenza and COVID (inference, not sourced). Drug screens, background checks and BLS/ACLS vary by institution and no cross-program official source was found, so follow your GME office's checklist [CDC](https://www.cdc.gov/mmwr/volumes/68/wr/mm6819a3.htm).

**Apply for your NPI** (`predep-05`) — The NPI is free; apply online through NPPES in about 20 minutes, and update NPPES within 30 days of any change [CMS](https://www.cms.gov/regulations-and-guidance/administrative-simplification/nationalprovidentstand/downloads/npi-requirements-for-prescribers.pdf). Programs usually ask for it at onboarding once you have a US address and ideally an SSN (timing unverified).

**Skip a personal DEA registration** (`predep-06`) — A hospital-employed practitioner may prescribe under the hospital's DEA registration with a hospital-assigned suffix, if the state permits it [21 CFR 1301.22](https://www.law.cornell.edu/cfr/text/21/1301.22). A personal DEA matters mainly after residency.

**Plan transfers from India under LRS** (`predep-07`) — Residents can remit up to USD 250,000 per financial year; forex-card loads count toward the limit [ICICI](https://www.icici.bank.in/nri-banking/nriedge/nri-articles/lrs-for-sending-money-abroad-from-india). The first ₹10 lakh a year is TCS-exempt; above that TCS is 2% for education or medical purposes and 20% for other purposes, creditable against income tax (rates from 1 Apr 2026) [ICICI](https://www.icici.bank.in/nri-banking/nriedge/nri-articles/lrs-for-sending-money-abroad-from-india). Splitting transfers across family members' PANs or across two financial years reduces TCS cash tied up (inference).

**Know the border cash rule** (`predep-08`) — You may bring any amount into the US, but more than $10,000 in total (cash, traveler's checks, money orders, bearer instruments) must be reported on FinCEN 105 [CBP](https://www.cbp.gov/travel/international-visitors/kbyg/money). India's limit on carrying currency notes out (commonly quoted as USD 3,000) is unverified (verify).

**Get an Indian International Driving Permit** (`predep-09`) — An Indian IDP is valid 1 year and costs about ₹1,000–1,500; apply via Parivahan Sarathi (Form 4A) or your RTO, typically within 3–7 days, with your licence, passport, visa, ticket and Form 1A medical certificate (secondary, verify on Parivahan) [Spinny](https://www.spinny.com/blog/international-driving-licence-apply-eligibility-cost/). Whether it lets you drive in California, and for how long, is unverified.

**Book a flight that lands no earlier than 30 days before start** (`predep-10`) — You may enter the US no earlier than 30 days before the DS-2019 start date [Intealth](https://certification.ecfmg.org/evsp/applying-pre-arrival.html). A planning estimate for the flight is $700–1,200 (unsourced estimate). Leave room for orientation, which often starts before 1 July.

**Line up housing near the hospital** (`predep-11`) — HUD FY2027 1-bedroom Fair Market Rents range from about $1,050 (Cleveland) to $3,003 (San Francisco) (secondary transcription; verify at huduser.gov) [StartBusinessByState](https://startbusinessbystate.com/?p=20015). Budget a deposit plus first month of about 2–3× monthly rent (unsourced estimate).

**Pack a hand-carry document folder** (`predep-12`) — Hand-carry passport, J-1 visa, signed DS-2019, I-901 receipt, contract or offer letter and insurance proof; do not check documents in baggage [Intealth](https://ecfmg.org/evsp/during-travel.html) [Intealth](https://certification.ecfmg.org/evsp/applying-pre-arrival.html). Also carry original MBBS degree, transcripts, ECFMG certificate, Step score reports and vaccination/TB records (inference).

## State training licenses

| State | Credential | Fee | Processing | Key points |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| New York | None for ACGME residents | Limited permit $105 if needed | — | Full IMG license: 3 yrs GME, Steps 1–3, $735 [NYSED](https://www.op.nysed.gov/professions/physicians/license-requirements) |
| Texas | Physician-in-Training permit | $200 + $7 | 4-week minimum review; ~14 business days simple; 90–120 days with formal review | Needs TMB ID# from GME; no outside moonlighting [TMB](https://www.tmb.texas.gov/apply-renew/physician/physician-apply/physician-in-training-permit-application) |
| Illinois | Temporary medical license | $230 | 6–8 weeks | Rush 2026 deadline 8 Apr; needs ED-NON form, ECFMG certificate, notarized translations [Rush GME (institutional)](https://www.rushu.rush.edu/sites/default/files/2026-02/Initial%20Temporary%20Medical%20License%20Checklist__with%20FAQ_2026.pdf) |
| California | PTL | Not re-verified | Within 180 days of start | [MBC](https://www.mbc.ca.gov/Licensing/Physicians-and-Surgeons/Apply/) |

## Hand-carry list

Passport · J-1 visa · signed DS-2019 · I-901 receipt · contract or offer letter · insurance proof [Intealth](https://ecfmg.org/evsp/during-travel.html); plus original MBBS degree, transcripts, ECFMG certificate, Step score reports and vaccination/TB records (inference).

## Common mistakes

- Insurance that starts after the DS-2019 start date or has a gap [Intealth](https://certification.ecfmg.org/evsp/applying-pre-arrival.html).
- Arriving more than 30 days before the DS-2019 start [Intealth](https://certification.ecfmg.org/evsp/applying-pre-arrival.html).
- Missing an Illinois or Texas training-license deadline — training cannot start without it [Rush GME](https://www.rushu.rush.edu/sites/default/files/2026-02/Initial%20Temporary%20Medical%20License%20Checklist__with%20FAQ_2026.pdf) [TMB](https://www.tmb.texas.gov/apply-renew/physician/physician-apply/physician-in-training-permit-application).
- Packing original documents in checked baggage [Intealth](https://ecfmg.org/evsp/during-travel.html).
- Remitting large sums as “other purpose” without planning for 20% TCS [ICICI](https://www.icici.bank.in/nri-banking/nriedge/nri-articles/lrs-for-sending-money-abroad-from-india).
